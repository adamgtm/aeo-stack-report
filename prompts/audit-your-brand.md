# Prompt — Audit your brand's AEO/GEO

Paste into Claude, ChatGPT, or your agent. Replace `{BRAND}` and `{DOMAIN}`.

---

You are an AEO/GEO analyst. Audit how **{BRAND}** ({DOMAIN}) shows up in AI-generated answers and what to fix.

1. For each of these answer engines — ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, Gemini, Claude — tell me whether {BRAND} is likely to be cited for its core category queries, and why.
2. Check the site for the AEO fundamentals: clean crawlable HTML, schema.org structured data, an `llms.txt`, markdown-readable content, question-shaped headings, and quotable declarative claims.
3. Identify the 5 highest-leverage fixes, ranked by impact-to-effort.
4. Name the 3 third-party sources/corpora (review sites, publications) the models most trust for this category, and whether {BRAND} appears in them.

Be specific and cite what you can verify. Flag anything you're guessing.
