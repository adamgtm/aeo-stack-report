## The Solution & Approach

Clay is an AI-native GTM data platform. It started in 2017 as programmable spreadsheets that pull live data from the internet, repositioned in 2022 around outbound data enrichment for sales and growth teams, then moved into AI agents for GTM with the 2023 launch of Claygent, its web-research agent. As of mid-2026 it increasingly positions itself as a callable function layer invoked from inside Claude, ChatGPT, and OpenAI Codex through Clay Functions, a tool the agent calls rather than a UI a human opens.

Clay is widely credited with originating the GTM Engineer role. As Andy Eung put it, "In 2024, Clay created the GTM engineer." The company builds the labor pipeline around that identity through the AlphaForge training bootcamp, the Clay Cup competition, and the GTM Talent Hub. Its "Clay runs on Clay" case-study series, covering ABM, recruiting, SEO and AEO, ads, and closed-lost analysis, publishes dogfooded playbooks that competitors cannot easily match.

Its distribution arc rides the LLM rails. Clay shipped inside ChatGPT in December 2025, inside Claude in January 2026, unified Clay MCP across Claude and ChatGPT in April 2026, and shipped inside OpenAI Codex in June 2026. Each move pulled agentic capability down a plan tier while widening the user surface. It is repeatedly named by operators as the platform that standalone sales-tech tools cannot compete with, and sits at the center of the AI GTM consolidation as the thriving counter-force to the acquisition wave.

## Company, Financials & Funding History

Kareem Amin and Nicolae Rusan founded Clay in June 2017 in New York around the idea of programmable spreadsheets that pull live data from the internet. Varun Anand joined as a co-founder in 2021. Clay took six-plus years to reach its first $1M in revenue and about two more to grow from $1M to $100M ARR, reaching a $5B valuation by early 2026.
