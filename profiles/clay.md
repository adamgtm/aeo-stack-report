## The Solution & Approach

Clay combines data from 200+ providers, AI research agents and workflow orchestration. Audiences maintains account and contact data; Workflows connects triggers, conditional logic and agents with tracing for execution and cost. Its AEO relevance is documented in its own [SEO and AEO playbook](https://www.clay.com/blog/how-clay-uses-clay-for-seo-and-aeo), positioning it as infrastructure for custom builds.

Clay describes itself as “the infrastructure GTM engineers build on.” Its API, CLI and Agent Plugin let coding agents search data, run enrichment and build workflows. The [Skills Marketplace](https://www.clay.com/blog/introducing-clay-skills-marketplace) packages community GTM playbooks for those agents. Installation is free; skills that call Clay data consume credits.

The latest direction is a “self-learning revenue engine.” At Sculpt 2026, Clay previewed an agent-first build experience while Workflows remained in beta. Its [internal learning experiments](https://www.clay.com/blog/gtm-self-learning-loops) describe agents proposing changes, people approving them and deployment updating production. Independent performance results are not provided.

## Company, Financials & Funding History

Kareem Amin and Nicolae Rusan founded Clay in 2017 in New York; Varun Anand joined as co-founder in 2021. The company began with programmable spreadsheets, focused on GTM data enrichment in 2022 and subsequently expanded into agents and workflow orchestration.
