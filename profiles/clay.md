## The Solution & Approach

Clay is an AI-native sales tech platform that started as programmable spreadsheets pulling live data, repositioned in 2022 to outbound data enrichment for sales and growth teams, and moved to AI agents for GTM with Claygent in 2023. Its stated goal is to be the default tool for GTM Engineering. It crossed $100M ARR in December 2025, carries a $5B valuation, and reported 452K active users and 2.4B+ AI runs in its 2025 recap.

The distribution angle is to ride the LLM rails: Clay shipped inside ChatGPT (December 2025), inside Claude (January 2026), unified its MCP server across Claude and ChatGPT on all modern plans (April 2026), opened a natural-language Claygent Builder free on every plan (May 2026), and launched inside OpenAI Codex (June 2026). Each move pulls agentic capability down a plan tier and makes Clay's data and workflows callable from inside the LLM rather than the Clay UI. Alongside this, the "Clay runs on Clay" series publishes first-party dogfooded playbooks (ABM, SEO/AEO, ads, recruiting, closed-lost) as content.

On category POV, Clay is widely credited with originating the GTM Engineer role in 2024 and owns the identity layer around it through the AlphaForge training bootcamp, the Clay Cup competition, and the GTM Talent Hub. Adam Schoenfeld names it "the $5B AI GTM leader," and it is positioned as the thriving counter-force to the AI GTM consolidation wave rather than a target of it.

## Best for

GTM and ops teams that want a programmable enrichment, scoring, and outbound layer they can build agents and custom workflows on top of, rather than a fixed point tool.

## Company, Financials & Funding History

Kareem Amin and Nicolae Rusan founded Clay in June 2017 in New York around the idea of programmable spreadsheets that pull live data from the internet; Varun Anand joined as co-founder in 2021. The company crossed $100M ARR in December 2025 and reached a $5B valuation, which Anand framed as "a true 8-year overnight success story."
