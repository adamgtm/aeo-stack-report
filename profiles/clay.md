## The Solution & Approach

Clay is an AI-native GTM data platform. It began in 2017 as a programmable spreadsheet that pulls live data from the web, repositioned in 2022 into outbound data enrichment for sales teams, then in 2023 launched Claygent, an AI research agent that moved the product from a data-enrichment workbench toward agents for go-to-market. Today Clay positions itself as the data substrate underneath GTM motions: import records from a CRM, warehouse, or its own marketplace into one layer that stays fresh, layer on first-party data, enrichments, and signals, then act on scoring, outbound, and ads. Claygent runs 350 million GTM agents a month.

The current angle is distribution through LLM surfaces. Clay increasingly presents itself as a callable function layer invoked from inside Claude, ChatGPT, and OpenAI Codex through Clay Functions, a tool the agent calls rather than an interface a human opens. The arc is deliberate: ChatGPT in December 2025, Claude in January 2026, a unified MCP server on all modern plans in April 2026, and OpenAI Codex in June 2026, each move pulling agentic capability down a plan tier and widening the user surface.

Clay also anchors the GTM Engineering category and is widely credited with originating the GTM Engineer role in 2024. It builds the surrounding labor pipeline through the AlphaForge training bootcamp, the GTM Talent Hub, and the Clay Cup competition, and publishes dogfooded 'Clay runs on Clay' playbooks (ABM, SEO and AEO, recruiting, closed-lost) as a content moat competitors cannot easily match. Operators name it as the consolidation leader of the AI GTM stack, at a $5B valuation with enterprise net revenue retention above 200%.

## Company, Financials & Funding History

Clay was founded in June 2017 in New York by Kareem Amin and Nicolae Rusan around the idea of programmable spreadsheets that pull live data from the internet, with Varun Anand joining as a co-founder in 2021. The company took six-plus years to reach its first $1M in ARR, then roughly two years to grow from $1M to $100M (crossed in December 2025), reaching a $5B valuation by early 2026.
