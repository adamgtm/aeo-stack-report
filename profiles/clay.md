## The Solution & Approach

Clay started in 2017 as a programmable spreadsheet and became the enrichment tool of choice for outbound teams. It now describes itself as "Infrastructure to get any data, run agentic workflows, and launch GTM plays." The 2026 releases moved it from rows to systems. Audiences replaced the 50,000-row table ceiling with a data layer that refreshes on its own. Workflows added a node-based orchestration canvas. Account Research Agents write findings back to the CRM. Claygent, its research agent, has passed 5 billion cumulative runs.

The distribution bet is to meet users inside other tools. Clay runs in ChatGPT, Claude, Codex, Microsoft Copilot, and Glean. Its API, CLI, and Agent Plugin let coding agents build and run Clay workflows from the terminal. That answers a real threat. Practitioners such as Brendan Short have described early Clay power users moving their work into Claude Code.

Clay also shapes the vocabulary around the GTM engineer, a role it is widely credited with creating. It runs the AlphaForge bootcamp, a GTM Talent Hub, the Clay Cup competition, and a $1,000,000 scholarship fund for people training into the role. For AEO work, Clay is a build option rather than a dedicated tool. It has published its own SEO and AEO playbook run on Clay.

## Best for

GTM engineering and ops teams that want to build their own data, research, and outbound systems on one enrichment and orchestration layer instead of buying a packaged tool.
