## The Solution & Approach

Clay combines third-party data, research agents and workflow orchestration so teams can build their own GTM systems. Its AEO application is documented in its own [SEO and AEO playbook](https://www.clay.com/blog/how-clay-uses-clay-for-seo-and-aeo), published in April 2026. That establishes internal use, with no independently measured AEO outcome supplied.

Audiences supplies continuously refreshed records, while Workflows connects triggers, conditional logic and agents with run tracing and cost visibility. The API, CLI and Agent Plugin let coding agents build and operate these systems from outside Clay's interface. Workflows remained in beta in October 2026.

Clay describes itself as “the infrastructure GTM engineers build on.” Its training programs, scholarship fund and Skills Marketplace support that positioning. The newer self-learning revenue-engine pitch adds agents that propose improvements, with human approval before production deployment in Clay's published internal experiments.

## Best for

GTM engineering and marketing operations teams that want to build custom AEO research and automation workflows and can own their configuration, evaluation and ongoing operation.

## Pricing & trial

Clay charges for actions and data credits, with Launch at $167/month, Growth at $446/month and custom Enterprise pricing; a free plan includes 500 actions and 100 data credits monthly with no card required, and annual billing saves 10%. [Pricing](https://clay.com/pricing).
