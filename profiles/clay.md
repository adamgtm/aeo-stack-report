## The Solution & Approach

Clay is the AI-native sales tech platform positioned at the center of the AI GTM Consolidation narrative, the counter-force to the sales-tech acquisition wave. It crossed $100M ARR in December 2025 and carries a $5B valuation on $202M raised, with 452K active users and 2.4B+ AI runs reported. Founded in 2017 as a programmable spreadsheet, it became a data-enrichment workbench with Claygent in 2023, then a full GTM-execution platform with Sequencer, Web Intent, Audiences, Functions, and MCP.

The distribution angle rides the LLM rails. Clay launched inside ChatGPT in December 2025, inside Claude in January 2026, unified both via Clay MCP in April 2026, and shipped Claygent Builder in May 2026, each move pulling agentic capability down a plan tier rather than gating it to Enterprise. Clay is also one of six companies in OpenAI's Frontier program.

Clay's category POV is GTM Engineering. Andy Eung's framing, 'In 2024, Clay created the GTM engineer,' captures the claim, and Clay now operates the full identity layer around it: the GTM Talent Hub, the AlphaForge training bootcamp, and the Clay Cup competition. It backs the position with dogfooded 'Clay runs on Clay' playbooks for ABM, SEO/AEO, ads, and recruiting, content a competitor cannot easily match.

## Best for

Teams that want a single AI-native platform spanning enrichment, scoring, outbound execution, and agentic workflows, especially those building a GTM-engineering function instead of buying point tools.

## Company, Financials & Funding History

Kareem Amin and Nicolae Rusan founded Clay in June 2017 in New York around the idea of programmable spreadsheets that pull live data from the internet. Varun Anand joined as a co-founder in 2021. The company took 6+ years to reach its first $1M and about two more to grow from $1M to $100M ARR.

| Date | Round | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| 2017-06 | Seed | $2.5M (First Round Capital), at founding |
| 2024-06 | Series B | $46M at $500M valuation (Meritech-led, with Sequoia, First Round, Box Group, Boldstart); $62M total, also disclosed a prior $13.5M Series A from Sequoia |
| 2025-01 | Series B extension | $40M at $1.25B valuation (Meritech-led); $102M total capital |
| 2025-05 | Employee tender | $20M at $1.5B valuation (Sequoia-led) |
