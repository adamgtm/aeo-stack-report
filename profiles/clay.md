## The Solution & Approach

Clay combines third-party enrichment, first-party customer data, research agents, and workflow execution. Audiences maintains the data layer; Workflows connects triggers, conditional logic, agents, and actions with per-run tracing and cost visibility. Its Agent Plugin lets coding agents build and edit those workflows.

Its documented AEO connection is the first-party playbook [How Clay Uses Clay For SEO And AEO](https://www.clay.com/blog/how-clay-uses-clay-for-seo-and-aeo). That supports its placement as a Build option for teams assembling their own processes. The published account provides no quantified AEO outcome.

Clay describes itself as “the infrastructure GTM engineers build on” and supports that positioning through training, scholarships, and community playbooks. At Sculpt in October 2026, it previewed a system that evaluates and improves revenue plays. The self-learning claim remains vendor-reported, without an independent performance measure.

## Best for

GTM engineering and marketing operations teams with the capacity to build and maintain custom research, enrichment, and AEO workflows.

## Pricing & trial

Pricing combines monthly subscriptions with action and data-credit allowances: Free includes 500 actions and 100 data credits per month with no card required, Launch starts at $167 per month, Growth at $446, and Enterprise is custom; annual billing saves 10%. [Pricing](https://clay.com/pricing).

## Company, Financials & Funding History

Kareem Amin and Nicolae Rusan founded Clay in New York in 2017 around programmable spreadsheets; Varun Anand joined as co-founder in 2021. The company focused on GTM data enrichment in 2022 and subsequently expanded into research agents, persistent audiences, and workflow orchestration.
