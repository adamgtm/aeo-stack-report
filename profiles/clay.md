## The Solution & Approach

Clay is an AI-native GTM data orchestration platform: 150+ enrichment sources feeding Claygent, its AI research agent, which now runs roughly 350 million GTM agents a month. On July 2, 2026 it refreshed its homepage for the first time in 18 months and repositioned from a data-enrichment tool to 'the infrastructure GTM engineers build on,' framed across five verbs: data, agents, orchestration, execution, and governance.

The angle is Clay as a callable function layer rather than a UI a human opens. Its July 9 API, CLI, and Agent Plugin run Clay from inside Claude Code, Codex, and Cursor, and the July 16 'Clay for Reps' launch surfaces Clay logic inside Claude, ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot, and Glean. RevOps builds and approves the workflow logic; reps consume it inside the AI tools they already use.

Clay is widely credited with originating the GTM engineer role and has built the category around it through its AlphaForge training bootcamp, the Clay Cup competition, and a GTM Talent Hub. It sits at the center of the AI GTM consolidation story as the counter-force to the acquisition wave, named by operators as the platform standalone sales-tech tools cannot match.

## Best for

RevOps and GTM engineering teams that want to build custom enrichment and agentic workflows on a live data substrate, then make that approved logic callable by reps from inside their own AI tools.

## Company, Financials & Funding History

Clay was founded in June 2017 in New York by Kareem Amin (CEO) and Nicolae Rusan, around the idea of programmable spreadsheets that pull live data from the internet; Varun Anand joined as co-founder in 2021. Amin has framed the trajectory as an '8-year overnight success': 6+ years to reach the first $1M in ARR, then roughly two years to grow from $1M to $100M, and now a $5B valuation.
