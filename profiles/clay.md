## The Solution & Approach

Clay combines data enrichment, research agents and workflow orchestration. Audiences holds continuously refreshed records; Workflows connects triggers, conditional logic and agents. Its AEO relevance comes from using those building blocks for custom processes, documented in its own [SEO and AEO playbook](https://www.clay.com/blog/how-clay-uses-clay-for-seo-and-aeo). The supplied research establishes an internal use case, with no quantified AEO customer outcome.

Clay describes itself as “the infrastructure GTM engineers build on.” Its API, CLI and Agent Plugin let teams build and operate workflows from coding agents, while MCP exposes approved workflows to reps inside chat. Training programs and a Skills Marketplace support the GTM engineer role around the product. The October preview extends that positioning toward a self-learning revenue system, with the agent-first build experience in early access, Workflows in beta and the Inbound SDR Agent on a pre-beta waitlist.

## Best for

GTM engineering and marketing operations teams with the capacity to build and maintain custom AEO research workflows alongside broader enrichment and revenue processes.

## Pricing & trial

Usage-based plans separate Actions from Data Credits: Free includes 500 Actions and 100 Data Credits monthly with no card required, Launch starts at $167/month, Growth at $446/month and Enterprise is custom; annual billing saves 10%. [Pricing](https://clay.com/pricing).

## Company, Financials & Funding History
