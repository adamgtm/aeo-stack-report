## The Solution & Approach

Graphite started as a growth-marketing agency and turned its SEO practice into an AI-driven topical-SEO and AEO/GEO product. In 2024 it launched a self-service Topical SEO platform, a Topic Graph database with topical-authority scoring and an Internal Links API, on top of the earlier agency model. Beta client Fourthwall reported 2,301% traffic growth and a 936% SEO revenue increase in nine months.

The marketing runs on research. Its 'Five Percent' series uses Similarweb panel data to make a contrarian case: AI search is far bigger than most analysts assume, roughly 45 billion monthly sessions and 56% of search engine volume, yet organic search is not collapsing, declining only 2.5% year over year in 2025. The 56% figure drew a public rebuttal from Rand Fishkin of SparkToro, turning the study into a methodology fight covered by Search Engine Land.

Founder Ethan Smith is one of the canonical AEO operator voices. He laid out the playbook on Lenny's Podcast ('ChatGPT traffic converts six times better than Google'), co-anchored AEO Conf 2026 alongside AirOps and Webflow, and frames the discipline around an 'Owned AEO' versus 'Earned AEO' split.
