## The Solution & Approach

Graphite started as a boutique growth-marketing agency and turned its SEO practice into a product. In May 2024 it launched a self-service Topical SEO platform: a Topic Graph database, topical-authority scoring, real-time monitoring, and an Internal Links API. Beta client Fourthwall reported 2,301% traffic growth and a 936% SEO revenue increase in nine months. The agency model still runs underneath, which is why this sits on the Hire side of the AEO/GEO market rather than the Buy side.

The research engine is doing as much work as the product. The Five Percent series publishes original studies built on Similarweb panel data and large keyword samples, with named authors carrying PhDs. The findings tend to cut against consensus: organic search traffic fell only 2.5% year over year in 2025, not the 25-60% drops in industry commentary. AI assistants generate 45 billion monthly sessions, 56% of search engine volume, with 83% of that inside mobile apps everyone else was missing. Rand Fishkin publicly challenged the methodology on the second one and Ethan Smith answered back.

Graphite's category POV is that AEO measurement is not yet trustworthy. Its August 2026 piece named a specific defect: some prompt-tracking tools force ChatGPT to run a web search when collecting responses, which no user does. On prompts that don't naturally trigger search, visibility differs by 20 points on average. The recommendation is to let the model decide and report grounding rate per prompt. Worth noting Graphite sells AEO services while its rivals sell the tracking tools.
