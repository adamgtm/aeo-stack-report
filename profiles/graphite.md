## The Solution & Approach

Graphite combines SEO and AEO services with proprietary software. Its Topical SEO platform, launched in 2024, includes a Topic Graph database, topical-authority scoring, and real-time monitoring. An Internal Links API supports programmatic internal linking.

Founder Ethan Smith frames the practice around “Owned AEO” and “Earned AEO,” covering a company's own content and its presence in outside sources. Graphite's Webflow case study reports that LLM share of voice rose from 33% to 64% and the share of signups attributed to LLMs doubled from 4% to 8%.

Its Five Percent research series also challenges AEO measurement practices. An August 2026 study found that forcing ChatGPT to search changed visibility scores by 20 points on average for prompts that would not naturally trigger search. Graphite recommends letting the model decide whether to search and reporting the grounding rate for each prompt.
