## The Solution & Approach

Graphite (San Francisco) is a growth-marketing agency that turned its SEO practice into a self-service Topical SEO and AEO product. In 2024 it launched a SaaS platform built around a Topic Graph database, topical-authority scoring, and an Internal Links API, moving from a boutique agency-only model to self-service.

The company runs a research-as-marketing engine, the "Five Percent" series, built on Similarweb panel data. Founder Ethan Smith is one of the canonical AEO operator voices. He explained the playbook on Lenny's Podcast, citing that ChatGPT traffic converts six times better than Google and calling AEO the biggest shift in search since Google's Panda updates, and co-anchored AEO Conf 2026 with AirOps and Webflow.

Its studies push contrarian positions on the AEO debate: that AI search is far bigger than web-only analyses show (45 billion monthly sessions, 56% of search engine volume) and that organic search is not collapsing (down only 2.5% year over year in 2025). The 56% finding drew a public rebuttal from Rand Fishkin of SparkToro, and a June 2026 simulation found 79.6% of runs collapse when AI retrieves its own self-authored content.
