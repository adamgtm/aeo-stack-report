## The Solution & Approach

Graphite combines SEO and AEO services with proprietary topical-SEO software. Its self-service platform launched in May 2024 with a Topic Graph database, topical-authority scoring, and real-time monitoring. An Internal Links API supports internal linking.

Founder Ethan Smith frames the discipline around “Owned AEO” and “Earned AEO,” covering how brands appear in AI-generated answers. Graphite's Webflow case study reports that LLM share of voice rose from 33% to 64% and the share of signups attributed to LLMs doubled from 4% to 8%.

Its Five Percent research series supplies the category argument: organic search remains material, AI assistant usage extends beyond browser traffic, and prompt-tracking methods can distort visibility measurements. An August 2026 study reported an average 20-point visibility difference between forced and natural search conditions for prompts that do not naturally trigger search.
