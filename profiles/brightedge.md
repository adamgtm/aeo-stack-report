## The Solution & Approach

BrightEdge adds AEO capabilities to an enterprise SEO platform built since 2007. AI Hyper Cube tracks brand mentions, citations, competitors, and sentiment across ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity. Earlier Data Cube X capabilities focused on Google AI Overviews.

Its approach connects visibility measurement to enterprise outcomes. AI Catalyst Recommendations translates competitive visibility gaps into actions, while the AI Early Detection System attributes traffic, leads, and revenue to AI search. Agent Edge extends the platform into delivery, serving pages to verified AI agents at the CDN layer while leaving human-facing pages unchanged.

BrightEdge positions AI search as an additional surface within organic search. Its September 2025 research reported that AI surfaces accounted for under 1% of search, supporting a pitch that combines established SEO investment with new AI visibility and agent-access capabilities.

## Best for

Enterprise marketing and SEO teams that want AEO measurement, revenue attribution, and agent-access capabilities alongside an established organic-search platform.

## Company, Financials & Funding History

Jim Yu, formerly of Salesforce, and Lemuel Park founded BrightEdge in 2007 and lead it as CEO and CTO, respectively. Headquartered in Foster City, California, the privately held enterprise SEO company has expanded into AEO through AI Catalyst, AI Hyper Cube, and Agent Edge.
