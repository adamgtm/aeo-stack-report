## The Solution & Approach

BrightEdge is an enterprise SEO platform founded in 2007 and one of the longest-running in the category, serving 57% of the Fortune 500. It belongs on the AEO/GEO map not as a startup but as the case study of an established SEO vendor retrofitting its data platform for the answer-engine era.

The retrofit runs a consistent arc. It started with a 2023 Generative Parser, added AI capabilities inside Data Cube X for Google AI Overviews in late 2024, consolidated the bet under the AI Catalyst product line in April 2025, and in March 2026 launched AI Hyper Cube to track brand presence across ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity. Jim Yu has framed each step as incremental rather than disruptive.

The category POV is protect-the-base. BrightEdge's research drumbeat, from 'AI Darwinism' to data showing AI surfaces still account for under 1% of search, argues organic remains the cornerstone while the company sells the new visibility layer on top. Its enterprise differentiator is revenue attribution: the AI Early Detection System attributes traffic, leads, and revenue to AI search, the enterprise-buyer answer to AEO-native tools that lead with visibility tracking.

## Best for

## Company, Financials & Funding History
