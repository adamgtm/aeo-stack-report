## The Solution & Approach

Amplitude is a public product-analytics company entering AEO from the side no pure-play can match: it already owns the behavioral data. The core suite — Analytics, Experiment, Session Replay, Guides & Surveys — is unchanged. AI Visibility is the new layer on top, and the whole pitch is that it does not live in its own dashboard.

AI Visibility quantifies how often a brand shows up in AI answers across hundreds of prompts — ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews on standard plans, with Gemini and Claude on the Advanced tier. It scores share of voice, ranks you against competitors, runs sentiment (does the model recommend you or warn against you), and traces the prompts and source pages the models pull from. Then the part that matters: because it sits inside Amplitude's analytics graph, you can tie a visibility score directly to the traffic, conversion, and revenue it drove, and segment AI-sourced visitors through the funnel. That closes the AI-mention → conversion loop that standalone AEO trackers can only estimate.

The go-to-market is classic incumbent land-grab. AI Visibility is free for every Amplitude customer on every plan, and there's a free, limited version for non-customers. That free tool became one of Amplitude's fastest-adopted releases ever — 27,000+ brands have run it since launch. The free + conversion-attribution combination is the real story here: Amplitude is using its install base and its analytics graph as the moat, not a category-defining AEO POV.
