## The Solution & Approach

Peec AI tracks brand visibility, citations, and competitive positioning across AI search engines. Its measurement approach uses UI scraping to capture real user interactions. CEO Marius Meiners described the business as “more of a mid-market mass product,” with agency pricing and multi-client reporting supporting that focus.

The product has expanded from visibility measurement into crawl diagnostics, SKU-level shopping analytics, brand-perception scoring, and competitor-ad monitoring. October 2026 brought Peec Agent and redesigned Actions, pairing recommendations with evidence and helping users investigate and carry out content changes. Users choose which changes to publish.

Peec also publishes research on how answer engines retrieve, rerank, and cite sources. Its category position is that AEO requires understanding how engines select answers, alongside tracking mentions. The research team studies those selection mechanisms to inform recommendations as models change.

## Best for

Mid-market marketing teams and agencies managing multiple clients that need AI-search visibility tracking, competitor benchmarking, and prioritized recommendations.
