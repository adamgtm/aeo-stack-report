## The Solution & Approach

Peec AI tracks brand visibility, citations, sentiment, and competitors across AI search engines. Its approach uses UI scraping to capture user-facing answers, with regional benchmarking and support for 115+ languages. CEO Marius Meiners described it as "more of a mid-market mass product" in [Series A coverage](https://sifted.eu/articles/peec-ai-raises-21m-series-a).

The product has expanded from measurement into recommendations and execution. Actions prioritizes opportunities from visibility data; Peec Agent investigates recommendations and helps carry out content changes, with users choosing what to publish. Shopping analytics, crawler diagnostics, brand-perception scores, and competitor-ad monitoring extend coverage beyond brand mentions.

Peec also publishes research on how answer engines retrieve, rank, and cite sources. Its research team's stated premise is that short-term visibility tactics will lose effectiveness, making an understanding of engine behavior central to sustained AEO performance.

## Best for

Mid-market marketing teams and agencies managing multiple clients that need AI-search visibility tracking, competitive benchmarking, and prioritized recommendations.
