## The Solution & Approach

Peec AI is an AI search analytics platform that tracks brand visibility, citations, and competitor position across AI engines. ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews come on every plan; Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek, Grok, Microsoft Copilot, Llama, and Google AI Mode are à la carte paid add-ons (~€20-30 per engine/month). Its launch differentiator was UI scraping of real user interactions rather than the API-only sampling competitors use, with 115+ supported languages and regional competitor benchmarking.

The product has widened from a citation tracker into prescriptive Actions (a prioritized worklist of where competitors are winning), crawl-side Agent Analytics (Crawl Insights and Crawlability), an MCP server with read and write operations, and a Looker Studio connector for BI reporting.

The category POV is explicit. CEO Marius Meiners frames Peec as "more of a mid-market mass product" — two-thirds of customers are companies with 100-200 employees — and a formalized agency program (tiered pricing, partner directory, co-branded case studies) makes agencies a core distribution channel. That positions Peec as the volume play against enterprise-first Profound.

## Best for

Mid-market marketing teams (roughly 100-200 employees) and agencies managing multiple clients that want accessible, multi-engine AI-search measurement without enterprise pricing.

## Pricing & trial

Subscription tiered by prompt volume: €75/month for 25 prompts, €169/month for 100 prompts, enterprise from €424/month, with three base engines included and additional models sold as à la carte add-ons; API access is gated to the €499/month Enterprise plan. [Pricing](https://peec.ai/pricing).
