## The Solution & Approach

Peec AI is an AEO/GEO analytics platform that tracks how often, where, and why AI systems cite a brand across ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews, with Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek, Grok, Copilot, Llama, and Google AI Mode available as paid add-on engines. It strips traditional SEO features to concentrate on AI-search citation tracking, competitor benchmarking, and source-influence analysis, and differentiates on UI scraping of real user interactions rather than API-only sampling.

The company's stated angle is mid-market scale. CEO Marius Meiners frames Peec as "more of a mid-market mass product," with two-thirds of customers at 100 to 200 employees and a dedicated agency program for firms managing multiple clients. That positions it against enterprise-first Profound, betting that volume buyers are where AI-search measurement scales.

Over 2026 the product widened from a citation tracker into prescriptive Actions, Agent Analytics crawl tools, an MCP server, and a roadmap reaching into brand-perception analytics, commerce, and measurement. Peec also pushes a category POV through its own research, arguing in a 500,000-prompt study that Google AI Overviews is the most undertracked AI-search surface relative to its reach.

## Best for

Mid-market marketing teams and agencies that want accessible, multi-engine AI-search visibility tracking without enterprise pricing or per-seat fees.

## Pricing & trial

Self-serve subscription, from €75/month for 25 prompts and €169/month for 100 prompts up to a €499/month Enterprise tier that gates API access, with separate agency tiers; no free trial is referenced in the public sources. [Pricing](https://peec.ai/pricing).

## Company, Financials & Funding History

Peec AI was founded in early 2025 in Berlin by Marius Meiners (CEO, a former esports pro turned software engineer with PwC venture-deals experience), Tobias Siwonia (CTO, a serial AI/consumer-tech founder), and Daniel Drabo (CRO). The three met in Antler's Berlin Winter 2024 cohort and grew the company from launch to a company-confirmed $10M ARR and 70+ employees within about 16 months.
