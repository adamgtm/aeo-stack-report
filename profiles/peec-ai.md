## The Solution & Approach

Peec AI is an AEO/GEO platform that tracks how often, where, and why AI engines cite a brand. It covers ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews on every plan, with Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek, Grok, Copilot, Llama, and Google AI Mode sold as à la carte add-on engines, the broadest add-on model menu in the category. Its stated differentiator is UI scraping of real user interactions rather than API-only sampling, plus a data pipeline that filters raw query sets down to brand-related and purchase-related questions.

The product has widened from a citation tracker into prescriptive workflow. Actions converts visibility data into a prioritized worklist that flags where competitors are winning and suggests next steps, and Agent Analytics added crawl-side tools (Crawl Insights and Crawlability) that move it into the technical side of GEO. An MCP server connects to Claude, Cursor, and VS Code with read and write access to brand entities, and a Looker Studio connector streams live data into BI dashboards.

Peec's category POV is mid-market mass over enterprise. CEO Marius Meiners frames it as "more of a mid-market mass product," with two-thirds of customers at 100 to 200 employees, and the company leans on dedicated agency pricing and a partner program as a core distribution channel. That positions it as the accessible, volume-priced option against enterprise-first players like Profound.

## Best for

Mid-market marketing teams and agencies that want accessible, volume-priced AI-search visibility tracking across many brands or clients rather than an enterprise-first platform.

## Pricing & trial

Self-serve tiered: €75/month for 25 prompts, €169/month for 100 prompts, and enterprise from €424/month, with separate agency tiers and add-on engines at roughly €20 to €30 each; no free trial is documented. [Pricing](https://peec.ai/pricing).
