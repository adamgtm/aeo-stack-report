## The Solution & Approach

Siteimprove is a 20-year-old web-governance platform that retrofitted itself into AEO. The base product is accessibility, SEO quality, content compliance, and analytics for enterprise web teams. The AEO layer sits on top of that base rather than replacing it.

The retrofit ran through three moves. Siteimprove acquired MarketMuse in October 2024 for content intelligence, changed CEOs in March 2025 to Nayaki Nayyar of Securonix, then rebuilt as Siteimprove.ai in August 2025, an agentic content intelligence platform running on Amazon Bedrock with Amazon Nova models. Its Search agents target "search engines, LLMs, and AI discovery platforms." The AEO capability got a name in April 2026 at Adobe Summit: Advanced AEO Insights, tracking AI citations, share of answers, brand sentiment, competitive position, and revenue attribution across answer engines.

The direction since then is measure, then produce, then embed. June 2026 added a Content Writing and Optimization Agent with AEO insights built into the draft, plus a PDF Remediation Agent. Three weeks later Siteimprove shipped an MCP server that puts the Accessibility Agent inside Claude, Lovable, VS Code, and Figma, checking content while an AI tool generates it. Regulation is the pull underneath all of it: the European Accessibility Act, Section 508, and ADA Title II push the same buyer that the AEO story is aimed at, and the August 2026 FedRAMP Ready designation opens the federal channel where both pitches land on one desk.

## Best for

Large enterprise and public-sector web teams already running accessibility and SEO governance at scale, who want AI visibility measured in the same platform rather than bought separately.

## Company, Financials & Funding History

Founded in 2003 in Copenhagen by Morten Ebbesen, with Troels Rasmussen and Peter Jensen. Ebbesen ran it as CEO for roughly 18 years, expanding into the UK, US, Sweden, and continental Europe before moving to the board in 2021. Nayaki Nayyar, formerly of Securonix, took over as CEO in March 2025 and has led the agentic rebuild since.
