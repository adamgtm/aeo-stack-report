## The Solution & Approach

Siteimprove is a web-governance platform founded in 2003 in Copenhagen. Its core is accessibility, SEO quality, content compliance, and analytics for enterprise web teams. It is a 20-year incumbent adding AI-search visibility on top of an existing accessibility and SEO base, rather than an AEO-native entrant.

The AEO push ran through three moves: the MarketMuse content-intelligence acquisition in October 2024, a March 2025 CEO change to Nayaki Nayyar, and the August 2025 launch of Siteimprove.ai, an agentic content-intelligence platform built on Amazon Bedrock and Nova whose Search agents target LLMs and AI discovery platforms. AEO surfaced as a named product, Advanced AEO Insights, at Adobe Summit in April 2026, tracking AI citations, share of answers, and brand sentiment across answer engines.

The direction is measure, then produce, then embed. In June 2026 a Content Writing and Optimization Agent moved the platform from measurement into production, and an MCP server pushed the accessibility checks inside the AI tools generating content, into Claude, Lovable, VS Code, and Figma. That separates Siteimprove from AEO-native entrants like Profound: the governance business predates LLMs and would survive without them, while the AEO capability sits on top.

## Best for

Enterprise web teams that already need accessibility, compliance, and SEO governance at scale and want AI-search visibility folded into the same platform rather than bought as a separate AEO point tool.

## Company, Financials & Funding History

Siteimprove was founded in 2003 in Copenhagen, Denmark by Morten Ebbesen, with Troels Rasmussen and Peter Jensen. Ebbesen led as CEO for roughly 18 years, expanding into the UK, US, Sweden, and continental Europe before moving to a board role in 2021.
