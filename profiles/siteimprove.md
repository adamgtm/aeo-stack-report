## The Solution & Approach

Siteimprove is a 20-year-old web-governance incumbent, accessibility, SEO quality, content compliance, and analytics for enterprise web teams, that is layering AI-search visibility on top of its existing base rather than rebuilding from scratch. The retrofit ran through three moves: the MarketMuse content-intelligence acquisition in October 2024, a March 2025 CEO change to Nayaki Nayyar, and the August 2025 launch of Siteimprove.ai, an agentic content-intelligence platform built on Amazon Bedrock and Nova whose Search agents target LLMs and AI discovery platforms.

The AEO capability surfaced as a named product, Advanced AEO Insights, on April 20, 2026 at Adobe Summit. It tracks AI citations and citation frequency, share of answers, brand sentiment in AI-generated responses, competitive positioning, and revenue attribution tied to AI visibility across answer engines. CEO Nayaki Nayyar framed it bluntly: "Answer engines are rapidly becoming the first point of engagement for buyers, and visibility in these AI channels is no longer optional."

The positioning is the incumbent's: one unified platform for content compliance and content performance, sold into Global 2000 web teams that already run Siteimprove for accessibility and SEO. That separates it from AEO/GEO-native entrants like Profound, where the entire product assumes answer engines exist. Siteimprove is a Representative Vendor in the 2026 Gartner Market Guide for Answer Engine Optimization, the analyst marker of the SEO-to-AEO retrofit path.

## Company, Financials & Funding History

Siteimprove was founded in 2003 in Copenhagen, Denmark by Morten Ebbesen, alongside Troels Rasmussen and Peter Jensen. Ebbesen led as CEO for roughly 18 years, expanding into the UK, US, Sweden, and continental Europe before moving to a board role in 2021.
