## The Solution & Approach

Siteimprove is a web-governance platform founded in 2003 for accessibility, SEO quality, content compliance, and analytics across enterprise web teams. Its AEO entry is a retrofit on that base, not a ground-up build. It is the kind of incumbent the answer-engine optimization wave is layering onto rather than displacing.

The retrofit ran through three moves. First the MarketMuse content-intelligence acquisition in October 2024. Then a March 2025 CEO change to Nayaki Nayyar, formerly of Securonix. Then the August 2025 launch of Siteimprove.ai, an agentic content-intelligence platform built on Amazon Bedrock and Nova whose Search agents target LLMs and AI discovery platforms. The AEO capability surfaced as a named product, Advanced AEO Insights, on April 20, 2026 at Adobe Summit, tracking AI citations, share of answers, and brand sentiment across answer engines.

The POV is unification: Nayyar's framing is that Siteimprove.ai is the only enterprise platform delivering both content compliance and performance on one platform. That separates it from AEO-native entrants like Profound, where the entire product assumes answer engines exist. Siteimprove is classified AI-Transitioned. The core accessibility and SEO business predates LLMs and would survive without them, but the 2025-2026 direction is built around generative AI.

## Best for

Enterprise web teams that already run accessibility, SEO, and content-compliance governance through Siteimprove and want AI-search visibility layered onto that existing base rather than bought as a standalone tool.
