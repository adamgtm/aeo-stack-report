## The Solution & Approach

Siteimprove is a web-governance platform: accessibility, SEO quality, content compliance, and analytics for enterprise web teams. Founded in 2003, it is the kind of incumbent the AEO wave is retrofitting rather than displacing.

The retrofit ran through three moves. First the MarketMuse content-intelligence acquisition in October 2024. Then a March 2025 CEO change to Nayaki Nayyar, formerly of Securonix. Then the August 2025 launch of Siteimprove.ai, an agentic content-intelligence platform built on Amazon Bedrock and Nova, whose Search agents target search engines, LLMs, and AI discovery platforms. AEO surfaced as a named product, Advanced AEO Insights, on April 20, 2026 at Adobe Summit, tracking AI citations, share of answers, and brand sentiment across answer engines.

Siteimprove is classified AI-Transitioned. The core accessibility, governance, and SEO business predates LLMs and would survive without them, but the 2025 to 2026 product direction is built around generative AI. That separates it from AEO-native entrants like Profound, where the entire product assumes answer engines exist.

## Best for

Enterprise web teams already invested in accessibility, SEO quality, and content governance that want AI-search visibility added on top of an existing platform rather than bought from a standalone AEO tool.
