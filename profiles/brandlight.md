## The Solution & Approach

Brandlight is an enterprise platform for monitoring and shaping how brands show up in AI-driven discovery. It tracks how AI answers get generated across engines and verticals, surfaces the sources that shape those answers, and turns the signal into recommendations for marketing, PR, content, and media teams. The governance posture is enterprise-first: SOC 2 Type 2, a no-PII stance, SSO, and licensing-data provenance. [source](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/the-ai-market-shelf-just-got-bigger-brandlight-raises-30m-to-continue-leading-enterprise-cmos-into-the-era-of-ai-native-ads-302684759.html)

The category POV is that AI visibility is becoming a real ad market, not just a measurement problem. Brandlight frames the destination as the "AI Market Shelf" — and positions the product as a marketing operating system for AI as a channel, starting with AI search and extending into AI ads and agentic commerce. CEO Imri Marcus: "Brandlight was built to become a marketing operating system for AI as a channel, starting with AI search and now expanding into AI ads and commerce." [source](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/the-ai-market-shelf-just-got-bigger-brandlight-raises-30m-to-continue-leading-enterprise-cmos-into-the-era-of-ai-native-ads-302684759.html)

Brandlight self-describes as the "#1 AEO platform"; we treat that as a vendor claim, not a verified placement. No independent analyst grid has named Brandlight a leader as of this writing.

## Best for

Large enterprises and Fortune 500 brand teams that want an AI-visibility platform with enterprise governance baked in, and that are leaning into the shift from AI search visibility to AI-native ads and agentic commerce.

Brandlight exposes a RESTful API for enterprise integrations — data flows to Looker Studio, GA4, GSC, CRMs, and PR/outreach tools, with SSO and governance controls — but access is reserved for enterprise deployments and there is no public developer-docs URL, MCP server, SDK, or CLI. Among AEO vendors this is one of the thinner build-on-top stories today. [source](https://sat.brandlight.ai/articles/does-brandlight-offer-api-access-for-integrations)
