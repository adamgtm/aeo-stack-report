## The Solution & Approach

HubSpot is a CRM and marketing platform founded in 2006, a pre-AI category-definer (inbound marketing, then all-in-one CRM) now threading AI agents and assistants across sales, marketing, and service under the Breeze brand. ChatSpot (2023) and the Clearbit and Cacheflow acquisitions seeded the data-and-AI layer, Breeze (2024) packaged it, and the 2025-2026 run reframed the whole company around it.

In AEO specifically, HubSpot shipped the first answer-engine-optimization tool from a major platform at its April 2026 Spring Spotlight. It runs itself as the reference customer: CEO Yamini Rangan says HubSpot is "the #1 most visible CRM and our leads from AI engines are converting up to 3x better than other channels," and the company reports its AEO Agent drove 1,850% growth in qualified leads from AI-generated answers between Q1 2025 and Q1 2026.

The broader POV is the "agentic customer platform." Co-founder Dharmesh Shah frames the goal as full API parity for agents: "Agents can run on HubSpot. And agents can RUN HubSpot." The MCP server, an Agent CLI, and date-based API versioning back that up, and in 2026 HubSpot renamed its 15-year INBOUND conference to UNBOUND to mark the shift from inbound-marketing methodology to agentic platform.

## Best for
