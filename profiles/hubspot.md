## The Solution & Approach

HubSpot brings AEO into the marketing platform its customers already use. Its AEO product launched in April 2026, following the acquisition of XFunnel. AI-visibility measurement is "included at no additional cost" in Marketing Hub Professional and Enterprise, with 2,500 monthly answers. [Source](https://www.hubspot.com/products/marketing/aeo)

The commercial angle is distribution through the existing customer base. Standalone AEO starts at $45 per month billed annually, while eligible Marketing Hub customers pay no incremental subscription fee for the included allowance. HubSpot supports that offer with competitor comparisons and guides for specific marketing roles. Its broader Loop Marketing framework puts AEO within an ongoing marketing process spanning content, distribution, and iteration. [Source](https://www.hubspot.com/loop-marketing)

## Best for

Marketing teams already using HubSpot Professional or Enterprise that want AI-visibility measurement within their existing platform and can work within the included 2,500 monthly answers.

## Pricing & trial

Standalone AEO costs $45 per month billed annually for 25 tracked prompts, with a free AEO Grader; Marketing Hub Professional and Enterprise include 2,500 monthly answers at no additional cost, and an AEO trial is not documented. [Pricing](https://www.hubspot.com/pricing).

## Company, Financials & Funding History

Brian Halligan and Dharmesh Shah founded HubSpot on June 9, 2006. The Boston-based company grew from inbound marketing software into a public CRM platform and introduced Breeze in 2024 before launching its AEO product in April 2026.
