## The Solution & Approach

HubSpot is a CRM and marketing platform founded in 2006, now threading AI agents and assistants across sales, marketing, and service under the Breeze brand. In April 2026 it shipped the first AEO tool from a major platform, packaged inside Breeze alongside its Customer, Prospecting, and Content agents.

Its AEO angle leans on proprietary research and running itself as the reference customer. HubSpot published data that CRM buyers using AI search were 36% more likely to purchase, with AEO-optimizing companies generating 170% more MQLs and 82% more deals. CEO Yamini Rangan has said HubSpot is the most visible CRM in AI engines, with leads from those engines converting up to 3x better than other channels, and the company reports its AEO Agent drove 1,850% growth in qualified leads from AI-generated answers between Q1 2025 and Q1 2026.

AEO sits inside a larger bet on the agentic customer platform, summed up by CTO Dharmesh Shah as agents that can run on HubSpot and agents that can run HubSpot. HubSpot renamed its 15-year INBOUND conference to UNBOUND in 2026 to mark the move from inbound-marketing methodology to that platform.

## Best for

Teams already running, or seriously considering, HubSpot's CRM who want AEO measurement and optimization built into the platform their GTM already runs on, rather than a standalone tool.

## Company, Financials & Funding History

Founded June 9, 2006 in Boston by Dharmesh Shah (CTO) and Brian Halligan (CEO until 2021, now Executive Chairperson and a Sequoia Partner). Yamini Rangan has been CEO since September 2021. The company is public on the NYSE with roughly 8,000 employees and about 300,000 customers.
