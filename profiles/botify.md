## The Solution & Approach

Botify is an enterprise SEO platform built on first-party crawl and log-file data at scale. Full-site crawling plus LogAnalyzer, which reads raw server logs, sold to large e-commerce and publisher sites that need to understand how engines see millions of pages. Founded in Paris in 2012, it reports 500+ customers across 30 countries and ~$85M raised.

Since the ChatGPT era it has repointed that same data substrate at AI-answer visibility. The log files that once powered crawl-budget analysis now track GPTBot, ChatGPT-User, OAI-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, and ClaudeBot, and the AI Visibility product measures brand presence inside Google AI Mode, ChatGPT, and Perplexity. The pitch against newer prompt-scraping AEO tools is that Botify's measurement is rooted in a brand's real search footprint, grounded in first-party crawl, Google Search Console, and log data.

The category POV is the retrofit itself: an incumbent technical-SEO vendor using its log-file vantage point as both product and thought leadership, including an April 2026 analysis of ~7 billion log files showing OpenAI's web crawl tripled since GPT-5. With Agentic Feeds it has started to cross from measuring AI discovery to publishing structured product data for AI agents to consume.

## Best for

Large e-commerce and publisher sites with millions of pages that already need enterprise crawl and log-file SEO analysis and want AI-answer visibility built on the same first-party data foundation.

## Pricing & trial

Pricing not public. Botify is enterprise sales-led, with request-a-demo the only path into a negotiated, managed-services-inclusive contract, and no self-serve tier or free trial.

## Company, Financials & Funding History

Founded in Paris in 2012 by Adrien Menard (CEO), Thomas Grange, and Stanislas "Stan" Chauvin as a technical SEO analytics platform for large sites, built on log-file analysis and full-site crawling. Global expansion to London and New York followed in 2016, and the company is now headquartered in New York.
