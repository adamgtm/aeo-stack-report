## The Solution & Approach

Botify is an enterprise SEO platform that has spent the ChatGPT era retrofitting itself into an AEO/GEO platform. The core has always been first-party crawl and log-file data at scale, full-site crawling plus LogAnalyzer reading raw server logs, sold to large e-commerce and publisher sites that need to understand how search engines see millions of pages. The homepage now reads "be found everywhere consumers, bots, and agents search for your brand," and the company calls itself the leading agentic AI solution for online visibility.

The angle is the retrofit itself. The same log files that powered SEO crawl-budget analysis now track GPTBot, ChatGPT-User, OAI-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, and ClaudeBot, and the same crawl infrastructure feeds AI Visibility, a measurement product for brand presence inside Google AI Mode, ChatGPT, and Perplexity. The pivot is dated: GPTBot tracking added to LogAnalyzer in December 2024, the agentic-workflows launch in February 2025, and AI Visibility shipped October 2025.

Botify's category POV is that AI-answer measurement should be grounded in a brand's real search footprint, first-party crawl, Google Search Console, and log data, rather than scraping prompts. It pushes that contrast against the wave of AEO-native startups. With Agentic Feeds, announced March 2026, it also moves from measuring AI discovery into publishing AI-optimized structured data, adapting to agent-commerce protocols including OpenAI's Agentic Commerce Protocol and Google's Universal Commerce Protocol.

## Best for

Large enterprise e-commerce and publisher sites that already need crawl and log-file analysis at scale and want AI-answer visibility built on that same first-party data foundation.

## Pricing & trial

Pricing not public. Botify is enterprise sales-led with a request-a-demo funnel into a managed-services-inclusive contract, and no self-serve tier or free trial.

## Company, Financials & Funding History

Botify was founded in Paris in 2012 by Adrien Menard (CEO), Thomas Grange, and Stanislas "Stan" Chauvin as a technical SEO analytics platform for large sites built on log-file analysis and full-site crawling. It expanded to London and New York in 2016, with HQ now in New York and offices across London, Singapore, Sydney, and Tokyo.
