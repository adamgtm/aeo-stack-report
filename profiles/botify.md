## The Solution & Approach

Botify is an enterprise SEO platform that has spent the ChatGPT era retrofitting itself into an AEO/GEO platform. The core has always been first-party crawl and log-file data at scale, full-site crawling plus LogAnalyzer reading raw server logs, sold to large e-commerce and publisher sites that need to understand how search engines see millions of pages. The same log files that powered SEO crawl-budget analysis now track GPTBot, ChatGPT-User, OAI-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, and ClaudeBot, and the same crawl infrastructure now feeds AI Visibility, a measurement product for brand presence inside Google AI Mode, ChatGPT, and Perplexity.

The angle is the retrofit itself. Botify is one of the cleanest examples of an incumbent technical-SEO vendor repointing its existing data substrate at AI-answer visibility. Its pitch against the wave of prompt-scraping AEO startups is that its measurement is "rooted in a brand's real search footprint", first-party crawl, Google Search Console, and log data, "rising above scraping prompts."

The category POV it pushes is that incumbency in search data is an advantage, not a liability. Botify turned its log-file vantage point into AEO thought leadership with an April 2026 analysis of roughly 7 billion log files quantifying OpenAI's crawl tripling since GPT-5, and it has begun crossing from measuring AI discovery into publishing structured data for AI consumption with Agentic Feeds.

## Best for

Large e-commerce and publisher enterprises that already run technical SEO at scale and want AI-answer visibility measurement grounded in their own first-party crawl, log-file, and Search Console data rather than scraped prompts.

## Pricing & trial

## Company, Financials & Funding History

Botify was founded in Paris in 2012 by Adrien Menard (CEO), Thomas Grange, and Stanislas "Stan" Chauvin as a technical SEO analytics platform for large websites, built on log-file analysis and full-site crawling. It expanded to London and New York in 2016 and is now headquartered in New York with 200+ employees across offices in London, Singapore, Sydney, and Tokyo.
