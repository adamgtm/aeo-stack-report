## The Solution & Approach

Botify is an enterprise SEO platform, founded in Paris in 2012, that has spent the ChatGPT era retrofitting into an AEO/GEO platform. The core is first-party crawl and log-file data at scale, full-site crawling plus LogAnalyzer reading raw server logs, sold to large e-commerce and publisher sites that need to see how search engines and AI bots read millions of pages. The same log files that once powered crawl-budget analysis now track GPTBot, ChatGPT-User, OAI-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, and ClaudeBot.

Its angle against the newer prompt-scraping AEO startups is that its measurement is rooted in a brand's real search footprint, built on first-party data from crawls, Google Search Console, log files, and visits rather than scraped prompts. AI Visibility, shipped October 2025, measures brand presence across Google AI Mode, ChatGPT, and Perplexity, covering mentions, citations, sentiment, and competitive benchmarking; Botify reported 86% of clients had projects live with it at launch.

The product line now pairs measurement with publishing. AgenticCatalog, which reached general availability in the June 2026 release, turns product data such as reviews, Q&A, and page details into AI-optimized feeds for OpenAI and Google under the Agentic Commerce Protocol and Universal Commerce Protocol. The distribution model is enterprise sales-led and managed-services-heavy, the legacy enterprise-SEO motion rather than a PLG one.

## Best for

Large e-commerce and publisher sites already running technical SEO at scale that want AI-answer visibility measurement grounded in their own first-party crawl and log data, plus a publishing surface for agent-driven commerce.

## Pricing & trial

Enterprise sales-led with no public self-serve pricing and no free trial; the only path is request-a-demo into a negotiated, managed-services-inclusive contract.

## Company, Financials & Funding History

Botify was founded in Paris in 2012 by Adrien Menard (CEO), Thomas Grange, and Stanislas "Stan" Chauvin as a technical/enterprise SEO analytics platform for large websites, built on log-file analysis and full-site crawling. Global expansion to London and New York followed in 2016, and the company is now headquartered in New York.
