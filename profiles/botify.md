## The Solution & Approach

Botify is an enterprise SEO platform whose core has always been first-party crawl and log-file data at scale. Full-site crawling plus LogAnalyzer reading raw server logs, sold to large e-commerce and publisher sites that need to understand how search engines see millions of pages. Through the ChatGPT era it has retrofitted that substrate into AEO/GEO, with the homepage now reading "be found everywhere consumers, bots, and agents search for your brand."

The pivot is dated. Botify added GPTBot tracking to LogAnalyzer in December 2024, reframed its mission around agentic AI with the February 2025 launch (SmartContent, SmartIndex, SmartLink, Botify Assist), and shipped AI Visibility in October 2025 to measure brand presence across Google AI Mode, ChatGPT, and Perplexity. The same log files that powered SEO crawl-budget analysis now track GPTBot, ChatGPT-User, OAI-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, and ClaudeBot.

Its category POV is that measurement should be "rooted in a brand's real search footprint" of first-party crawl, GSC, and log data, "rising above scraping prompts." That positions it directly against the wave of prompt-scraping AEO startups, and it has turned its log-file vantage point into thought leadership, including an April 2026 analysis of ~7B log files quantifying OpenAI's crawl tripling since GPT-5.

## Best for

Large e-commerce and publisher sites with millions of pages that want AI-answer visibility grounded in their own first-party crawl and log data, plus heavy managed services.

## Pricing & trial

Pricing not public. Botify is enterprise sales-led, with request-a-demo into a negotiated, managed-services-inclusive contract and no self-serve tier or free trial.

## Company, Financials & Funding History

Founded in Paris in 2012 by Adrien Menard (CEO), Thomas Grange, and Stanislas "Stan" Chauvin as a technical/enterprise SEO analytics platform built on log-file analysis and full-site crawling. Global expansion to London and New York followed in 2016, with the company now headquartered in New York.
