## The Solution & Approach

daydream is an AI-native SEO and AEO/GEO firm that sells itself as an agency-as-product. SEO agents handle execution at scale, and every customer gets a dedicated human Growth Lead who owns strategy, quality, and outcomes. The pitch is the hybrid: not pure software, not a traditional agency, but services delivered through agents with a human expert in the loop. The homepage hero is "Your unfair advantage in SEO & AI Search."

The company started in 2023 as software automating programmatic SEO, then over 2025 added editorial (via the Positional acquisition), AEO/GEO monitoring (via a Scrunch AI partnership), and the Growth-Lead-per-customer staffing model to become a full-spectrum partner. Its V2.0 release in May 2025 formalized the shift from single-discipline SEO shop to multi-discipline agency-as-product.

daydream maps onto Y Combinator's Spring 2026 Request for Startups, which ranked AI-native agencies the #3 category it most wants founders to build: deliver the outcome yourself rather than sell a tool. In May 2026 it took its first step toward separating its software and services lines, launching Sunbeam, a fully autonomous SEO agent, as a standalone product under daydream Labs.

## Best for

Startups and growth teams that want organic search and AEO/GEO execution run by agents but with a dedicated human strategist owning the outcome, rather than buying a tool to operate themselves.
