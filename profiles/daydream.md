## The Solution & Approach

daydream is an AI-native SEO and AEO/GEO firm that packages itself as an "agency-as-product." SEO agents do execution at scale, and every customer gets a dedicated human "Growth Lead" who owns strategy, quality, and outcomes. The hybrid is the pitch: not pure software, not a traditional agency, but services delivered through agents with a human expert in the loop. Its homepage hero is "Your unfair advantage in SEO & AI Search," and it frames itself as executing faster than traditional agencies.

The model maps onto Y Combinator's Spring 2026 Request for Startups, which ranked AI-native agencies the #3 category YC most wants founders to build: businesses that deliver the outcome faster, cheaper, and better and capture the value themselves rather than selling a tool. daydream aims that thesis squarely at organic search and the shift toward answer engines, where ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Claude, not just Google, decide what gets cited.

The offering widened over 2025. daydream acquired Positional (YC S21) in April 2025 to add editorial SEO, partnered with Scrunch AI in March 2025 to bundle LLM/AI search visibility monitoring into every client engagement, and rolled both into a V2.0 launch spanning programmatic, editorial, technical, and AEO/GEO work under one roof. a16z later cited daydream alongside Profound and Goodie as platforms that help brands understand how they appear in AI-generated responses.

## Best for

Brands that want organic search and AEO/GEO outcomes delivered as a managed service, executed by agents but owned by a dedicated human strategist, rather than buying software to run themselves.

## Pricing & trial

Pricing not public. daydream is sold as a services engagement, routing to "Book a call" and a Full-Service qualification rather than self-serve tiers, with no free trial.

## Company, Financials & Funding History

daydream was founded in September 2023 in San Francisco by CEO Thenuka Karunaratne, who bootstrapped Flixed to 400K+ monthly visitors on programmatic SEO and was an Entrepreneur-in-Residence at Hustle Fund, and CTO Shravan Rajinikanth, an ex-Meta engineer who worked on Facebook Jobs and Horizon Worlds. It was built first as software to automate programmatic SEO end-to-end, with the agency-as-product packaging coming later.
