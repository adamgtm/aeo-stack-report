# daydream

daydream is an AI-native SEO and AEO firm built as an agency-as-product: SEO agents execute at scale with a dedicated human Growth Lead per customer. Customers include Twingate, Clay, and Beautiful.ai. $21M raised, capped by a $15M Series A led by WndrCo in April 2026, after a Shorty Award win for its OpenArt programmatic-SEO campaign.


## The Solution & Approach

daydream is an AI-native SEO and AEO firm built as an "agency-as-product." SEO agents do the execution at scale, and every customer is assigned a dedicated human "Growth Lead" who owns strategy, quality, and outcomes. The hybrid is the whole pitch: not pure software, not a traditional agency, but services delivered through agents with a human expert in the loop. Its own framing is to execute faster than traditional agencies.

The model maps cleanly onto Y Combinator's Spring 2026 Request for Startups, which ranked AI-native agencies the #3 category YC most wants founders to build, businesses that deliver the outcome faster, cheaper, and better and capture the value themselves rather than selling a tool. daydream aims that thesis squarely at organic search and the shift toward answer-engine optimization, where ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Claude, not just Google, decide what gets cited.

The proof points are programmatic SEO at scale. The OpenArt campaign reported 1M monthly visits in 8 months across 600+ programmatic pages, and won the Shorty Awards SEO & SEM category plus a Bronze in GEO, the cleanest public marker of daydream working the answer-engine surface. Twingate's CEO reports daydream-produced content now drives 40,000+ visits per month, or 80%+ of its non-branded traffic.

## Best for

B2B teams that want organic search and AEO outcomes delivered as a managed service, run by agents with a dedicated Growth Lead, rather than buying software to operate themselves.

## Pricing & trial

No public pricing. daydream is sold as a services engagement; the site routes to "Book a call," a Full-Service qualification, and a waitlist rather than self-serve tiers.
