# daydream

daydream is an AI-native SEO and AEO agency serving companies including Clay and Twingate. It has raised $21M and recently launched the autonomous Sunbeam product, expanded its full-spectrum V2.0 service, and added AI search monitoring through Scrunch AI.

## The Solution & Approach

daydream describes its model as an "agency-as-product." SEO agents execute work at scale, while a dedicated human Growth Lead owns strategy, quality, and outcomes for each customer. The firm sells an operating service rather than access to a conventional software dashboard.

The offering expanded from programmatic SEO into editorial, technical, and AEO/GEO work. The Positional acquisition added editorial capabilities, while the Scrunch AI partnership brought AI search visibility monitoring into standard client engagements.

daydream argues that AI-native agencies can deliver outcomes with software-like operating characteristics while retaining human accountability. Its May 2026 launch of Sunbeam, a separately branded autonomous SEO agent, began separating the managed-service and software product lines.

## Best for

Growth and marketing teams that want outsourced SEO and AEO/GEO execution with agent-driven production and a dedicated human lead responsible for strategy and quality.

## Company, Financials & Funding History

Founded in San Francisco in September 2023 by CEO Thenuka Karunaratne and CTO Shravan Rajinikanth, daydream began as software for automating programmatic SEO. It subsequently added editorial, technical, and AEO/GEO services, adopted a Growth-Lead-per-customer model, and launched Sunbeam as a separate software brand.
