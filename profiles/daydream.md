## The Solution & Approach

daydream packages itself as an "agency-as-product." SEO agents run execution at scale while every customer gets a dedicated human Growth Lead who owns strategy, quality, and outcomes. The pitch is the hybrid itself: software-speed execution with a named human accountable for the result. Its homepage hero is "Your unfair advantage in SEO & AI Search," and it describes itself as "a leading AI-native agency that delivers an unfair advantage in organic search with SEO agents and dedicated experts."

The model maps onto Y Combinator's Spring 2026 Request for Startups, which ranked AI-native agencies the #3 category it most wants founders to build, on the thesis that a service company can deliver the outcome faster, cheaper, and better and capture the value itself rather than selling a tool. daydream aims that thesis at organic search and the shift toward answer engines, where ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Claude, not just Google, decide what gets cited. It moved from programmatic-SEO-only to a full-spectrum offering with V2.0 in 2025, adding editorial SEO through the Positional acquisition and an AEO/GEO layer through a Scrunch AI partnership.

In 2026 daydream began separating its agency-facing and software-facing lines. In May it launched Sunbeam, a fully autonomous SEO agent sold as a standalone product under daydream Labs, aimed at seed-to-Series-A teams as a lower-cost alternative to a full-service retainer.

## Best for

Companies that want SEO and AEO/GEO execution run at software speed but still want a named human owning strategy and quality, rather than a self-serve tool or a traditional retainer agency.

## Pricing & trial

Pricing is not public. daydream is sold as a services engagement that routes to "Book a call" and a Full-Service qualification rather than self-serve tiers, with no free trial; its standalone Sunbeam agent is pitched as a lower-cost option for teams of 1 to 10.

## Company, Financials & Funding History

daydream was founded in September 2023 in San Francisco by CEO Thenuka Karunaratne, who bootstrapped Flixed to 400K+ monthly visitors on programmatic SEO and was an Entrepreneur-in-Residence at Hustle Fund, and CTO Shravan Rajinikanth, an ex-Meta engineer who worked on Facebook Jobs and Horizon Worlds. It started as software automating programmatic SEO end to end, then added the human Growth-Lead agency layer.
