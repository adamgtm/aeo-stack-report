## The Solution & Approach

Goodie AI is a commerce-vertical AEO/GEO platform. It tracks and improves how brands appear across ChatGPT and its Agentic Commerce surface, Amazon Rufus, Perplexity Shopping, Gemini, and Claude, pairing visibility monitoring (mentions, citations, and share of voice broken out by model, geography, and persona) with prioritized optimization guidance and attribution. It reports SOC 2 Type II compliance for enterprise buyers.

The pitch is that Goodie was built for generative search from the start, in contrast to SEO incumbents like Ahrefs, BrightEdge, and Botify retrofitting AEO onto legacy crawlers. Founder Mostafa ElBermawy came from the NoGood growth agency and has been speaking on AI search since SXSW 2023. The company bet early on agentic-commerce surfaces such as ChatGPT Shopping and Amazon Rufus before they were standard tracking targets, and supports OpenAI's Agentic Commerce Protocol and Google's Universal Commerce Protocol.

Goodie also runs a research-as-marketing motion its retrofit rivals lack. Its 2026 AI Search Traffic Report drew on an anonymized GA4 brand panel, SimilarWeb visit data, and SensorTower rankings to report that ChatGPT's share of B2B AI referrals fell from 89.1% to 62.6% over eight months while Claude rose from 1.4% to 18.5%. Customer-claimed results, such as a reported 127% AI-conversion lift for Dermalogica, are vendor-published and unaudited.

## Best for
