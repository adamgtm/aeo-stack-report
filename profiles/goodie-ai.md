## The Solution & Approach

Goodie AI combines visibility monitoring, optimization guidance, and attribution across ChatGPT, Amazon Rufus, Perplexity Shopping, Gemini, Claude, and other AI surfaces. Its commerce suite adds SKU-level monitoring, feed remediation, product-copy generation, schema injection, and AI attribution.

Its positioning centers on being built specifically for AEO/GEO rather than adding AI-search features to an established SEO platform. Goodie also distinguishes between visibility and accuracy: Brand Command checks model claims against approved brand facts and routes inaccuracies toward corrective action.

The company supports its category position with proprietary research, including the versioned AEO Periodic Table and studies based on prompt, citation, GA4, and SimilarWeb data.

## Best for

Commerce brands and marketing teams that need model-specific visibility tracking, optimization guidance, and product-level monitoring across AI search and shopping surfaces.

## Company, Financials & Funding History

Mostafa ElBermawy, also founder and CEO of growth agency NoGood, founded Goodie AI in New York in 2024. The bootstrapped company reported roughly $1.2M ARR, about 11 employees, and a $3.6M valuation as of December 2025, figures that have not been audited.
