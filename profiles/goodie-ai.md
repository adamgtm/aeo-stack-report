## The Solution & Approach

Goodie AI is a commerce-focused AEO/GEO platform that tracks and improves how brands appear across ChatGPT (including its Agentic Commerce surface), Amazon Rufus, Perplexity Shopping, Gemini, and Claude. It pairs AI-visibility monitoring, mentions, citations, and share of voice broken out by model, geography, and persona, with prioritized optimization guidance and attribution, and reports SOC 2 Type II compliance for enterprise buyers.

The positioning is that Goodie was built for generative search from the start, in contrast to SEO incumbents like Ahrefs, BrightEdge, and Botify retrofitting AEO onto legacy crawlers. It bet early on agentic-commerce surfaces, ChatGPT Shopping, Amazon Rufus, and Perplexity Shopping, before they were standard tracking targets, and shipped a SKU-level Agentic Commerce Optimizer in October 2025 with one-click feed remediation and AI attribution tracking.

Goodie leans on research-as-marketing that its retrofit rivals lack. It publishes the versioned AEO Periodic Table (V4 draws on 1.13 million prompts across six AI platforms) and a 2026 AI Search Traffic Report built from its own GA4 brand panel and SimilarWeb data. Founder Mostafa ElBermawy came from NoGood, the NYC growth agency, and has pushed the AI-search thesis publicly since SXSW 2023.

## Best for

Commerce and e-commerce brands that want to track and optimize product visibility across AI shopping surfaces like ChatGPT Shopping, Amazon Rufus, and Perplexity Shopping.
