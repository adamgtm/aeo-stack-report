# Scrunch AI

Scrunch AI is an AEO/GEO platform for measuring AI visibility and serving content to agents, with customers including Lenovo and RunPod; it raised $19M before Sitecore acquired it and recently introduced Site Diagnostics, Keyword Volume Estimator, and a reorganized Agent Experience Platform.

## The Solution & Approach

Scrunch combines AI-answer monitoring with page diagnostics, content optimization, and agent traffic measurement. Its product line spans Signals, Site Diagnostics, Optimizer, and Agent Traffic Monitoring, with a read-write MCP interface for querying data and initiating work.

Its distinguishing feature is content delivery. The original Agent Experience Platform integrates at the CDN layer to serve structured, machine-readable versions of pages to AI crawlers. Site Diagnostics extends the approach to teams without that integration, ranking technical, structural, and content fixes by predicted impact on retrieval and citation.

Scrunch frames AEO/GEO as an agent experience problem that extends beyond visibility reporting. Sitecore acquired the company in June 2026, and Scrunch has continued releasing products under its own brand.

## Best for

Brand and agency teams that want AI visibility measurement connected to page remediation, content optimization, and infrastructure for serving AI agents.
