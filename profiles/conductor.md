## The Solution & Approach

Conductor is a legacy enterprise SEO and content-intelligence platform (founded 2010, ~460 employees) that has retrofitted wholesale for AEO. It now calls itself the industry's "system of record for AEO," layering AI-visibility tracking, content-gap mapping, and autonomous agents onto its SEO core. Conductor AI, its first AI-visibility platform, launched in June 2025; the 2026 AI Search Performance release folded measurement, recommendations, and execution into one product.

The angle is AgentStack, the enterprise AEO suite launched at Adobe Summit in April 2026. It bundles three layers: native LLM apps that run inside ChatGPT, Claude, and Microsoft Copilot; developer infrastructure (APIs plus an MCP server); and turnkey AEO agents that need no prompt engineering. CEO Seth Besmertnik's pitch: "Once you connect Conductor's MCP to your AI platform or deploy our native connectors, you can build a custom version of our application in a day." Rather than sell standalone, Conductor bundles its AEO intelligence into partner CMS and DXP platforms, an OEM pattern it set with Acquia in February 2026 and extended through an exclusive Optimizely partnership in June.

Conductor is also manufacturing the demand narrative for the category. Its vendor-run "State of AEO/GEO in 2026" CMO report claimed 97% of digital leaders saw a positive business outcome from AEO in 2025 and that enterprises allocate 12% of digital budgets to it. Read the superlatives ("only system of record," "system of record for AEO") as positioning, not verified fact; CMSWire flagged Conductor's "90% reduction in reporting time" and "100x" content claims as not independently verified.

## Best for

Enterprise content and SEO teams with a large existing footprint that want AI-visibility measurement and turnkey AEO agents wired into their current CMS or DXP stack.
