## The Solution & Approach

Conductor is an enterprise SEO and content intelligence platform founded in 2010, now repositioning around AEO. Its April 2026 AgentStack launch bundles three layers: native LLM apps that run inside ChatGPT, Claude, and Microsoft Copilot; developer infrastructure (APIs plus an MCP server); and turnkey AEO agents that need no prompt engineering. The pitch is insight to published, optimized content in under three minutes.

Conductor is also manufacturing its category's demand narrative. Its State of AEO/GEO CMO Investment Report (97% of CMOs say AEO drove a positive 2025 outcome, 94% plan to increase 2026 investment) and its 2026 AEO/GEO Benchmarks Report (AI traffic at about 1.08% of sessions across 10 industries) are vendor-run, to be read with the commercial-interest flag attached.

The footprint underneath is built through SEO consolidation, acquiring ContentKing in 2022 and Searchmetrics in 2023, and Conductor is now selling AEO back into that install base. FY2026 results claim 50+ new enterprise logos and heavy upsell, all company-reported. It is the clearest example of a legacy enterprise-SEO platform retrofitting wholesale for AEO rather than adding a dashboard.

## Best for

Enterprise teams already standardized on SEO and content tooling who want AEO layered into an existing platform rather than adopting a separate AEO-native point tool.
