## The Solution & Approach

Bluefish AI (legal entity Bluefish Labs) is a New York enterprise AI marketing platform founded in 2024. It positions itself as "the AI marketing platform of choice for the Fortune 500," and the job is brand visibility, influence, and verification across AI channels including ChatGPT, Google AI, Claude, Perplexity, and Amazon Rufus, the AEO/GEO category.

Against peer Profound, Bluefish separates on protection rather than data depth. Its November 2025 AI Impact and Influence Analytics moved measurement past raw citation counts to an authority score, pairing an Impact Score (how closely a cited source aligns with the AI response) with an Influence Rank aggregated across thousands of responses. Its May 2026 AI Accuracy product extracts and verifies every factual claim AI channels make about a brand, traces each mismatch to the exact channel and response, and scores it by severity. A companion capability, Brand Vault, ingests first-party content into a source of truth shareable with LLMs.

The POV is that citation frequency alone overstates true influence, and that brand accuracy in AI answers is a protection problem worth instrumenting. Bluefish runs a steady research-report cadence (holiday, Black Friday, Super Bowl) that turns its own measurement corpus into public proof, positioning AI favorability as the next ROI metric for paid media.
