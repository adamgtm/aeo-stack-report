## The Solution & Approach

Bluefish is an enterprise AEO/GEO platform that tracks how AI channels such as ChatGPT, Google AI, Claude, Perplexity, and Amazon Rufus describe a brand. Its angle against peers is protection rather than data depth. AI Impact and Influence Analytics (November 2025) scores sources by how closely they shape AI answers, since citation counts alone overstate influence. AI Accuracy (May 2026) extracts every factual claim AI channels make about a brand, checks it against a first-party Brand Vault, and scores each mismatch by severity. Agentic Campaigns (June 2026) moved the product into orchestration: teams set AI performance targets, and the platform diagnoses content gaps and generates AEO tactics.

The category POV Bluefish pushes is methodology. Across three posts in ten days in July and August 2026, it argued that keyword and fixed-prompt tracking cannot measure AI visibility, and that controlled synthetic prompts across audience segments can. Its research reports on Black Friday, the holidays, the Super Bowl, and the State of Enterprise Brands in AI turn its own measurement corpus into public proof.

Distribution is widening. In August 2026, Omnicom PR agency MMC launched WEATHERVaiNE, its own branded product built on Bluefish's platform. In September, Bluefish partnered with Bazaarvoice to feed AEO insights into review campaigns. Both reach brands through another company's relationship.
