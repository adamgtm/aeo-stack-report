# AthenaHQ

AthenaHQ is an AEO/GEO measurement and remediation platform serving brands including Coinbase and SoFi, with approximately $2.7M in disclosed funding and recent releases spanning an official Claude connector, the Athena Citation Engine, and an agentic copilot for AI search visibility.

## The Solution & Approach

AthenaHQ combines AI search measurement with tools for changing what answer engines say about a brand. Its measurement layer tracks visibility and citations, draws on a catalog of 3M+ AI responses, and uses its Query Volume Estimation Model to estimate prompt demand where conventional keyword-volume data is unavailable.

The remediation layer includes Oracle for identifying incorrect brand claims and the Athena Citation Engine for assessing citation likelihood before publication. Athena AI, introduced in June 2026, replaced the dashboard as the primary interface. AthenaHQ's own ACE study covered 1,761 published articles and reported an association between higher scores and higher citation rates. It does not establish that optimization caused those outcomes.

Co-founder Andrew Yan advocates prompt portfolios organized by product, audience, buying stage, market, language, and engine. His positioning favors engine-specific AEO strategies. AthenaHQ's October benchmark commentary did not disclose enough sampling detail to establish general market baselines.

## Best for

Brand and content teams that want AI search monitoring, claim correction, and content optimization in one platform, with self-serve entry pricing and enterprise integrations.

## Pricing & trial

Self-Serve costs $95 per month with annual billing or $295 month to month for 3,600 credits, eight LLMs, and unlimited seats; Enterprise pricing is custom, and the published offer specifies a first-month discount rather than a free trial. [Pricing](https://athenahq.ai/pricing).

## Company, Financials & Funding History

Founded in 2024 and based in San Francisco, AthenaHQ was co-founded by Andrew Yan, formerly of Google Search, and Alan Yao. The company joined Y Combinator's Winter 2025 batch and emerged from stealth in June 2025 with 70+ customers.
