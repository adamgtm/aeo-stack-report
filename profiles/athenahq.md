## The Solution & Approach

AthenaHQ organizes its product around measuring AI-search visibility and acting on the findings. Its measurement layer combines citation tracking, a catalog of 3M+ AI responses, and QVEM, a proprietary model that estimates prompt volumes where conventional keyword-volume data is unavailable.

The action layer addresses brand accuracy and content production. Oracle flags conflicting claims for review. Athena Citation Engine scores drafts for citation likelihood before publication. The Athena AI copilot became the primary interface in June 2026. AthenaHQ's published ACE validation covered 1,761 articles, with top-decile content cited 87% of the time versus 38.6% for bottom-decile content. These are vendor-published findings. [Source](https://athenahq.ai/blog/the-science-of-ai-citation).

Co-founder Andrew Yan advocates organizing prompt portfolios by product, audience, buying stage, market, language, and engine. His broader argument is that citation strategies should vary by engine. AthenaHQ's latest benchmark commentary supports that positioning, but does not disclose enough methodology to establish general market baselines.

## Best for

Brand and marketing teams that want AI-search citation monitoring, brand-accuracy review, and content optimization in one platform, with self-serve entry pricing and enterprise controls.

## Pricing & trial

Self-serve costs $95 per month billed annually or $295 billed monthly, including 3,600 credits, eight LLMs, and unlimited seats; enterprise pricing is custom, a first-month discount is advertised, and no free trial is documented. [Pricing](https://athenahq.ai/pricing).

## Company, Financials & Funding History

Founded in 2024 in San Francisco by Andrew Yan, formerly of Google Search, and Alan Yao. AthenaHQ joined Y Combinator's Winter 2025 batch and emerged from stealth in June 2025 with 70+ customers and a $2.2M seed round.
