## The Solution & Approach

OtterlyAI is a bottom-up, product-led AI-search monitoring platform that positions itself as "Semrush for AI search." It tracks where a brand shows up, what is said about it, and which sites get cited across ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Google AI Mode, Perplexity, Gemini, and Microsoft Copilot. Its GEO Audit scores 25+ on-page factors, including content, page speed, structured data, and AI readiness, to tie monitoring to actionable optimization.

The company's angle is distribution through incumbents rather than against them. It launched inside the Semrush App Center in January 2025, and added bundled partnerships with Storyblok and PR platform Press Ranger. The product is the bootstrapped, self-serve floor of the AEO/GEO category, sitting against venture-funded peers Profound and Peec AI.

Its category POV is built on its own research: a study of 1+ million citations found that 95% of AI search citations draw from third-party sources rather than brand-owned content, and a separate study found YouTube is the #2 social platform for AI citations behind Reddit. CEO Thomas Peham frames the shift simply: "When people search with AI, they're not clicking links anymore. They're reading answers."

## Best for

Self-serve marketers, SEO professionals, and agencies who want bootstrapped, affordable AI-search monitoring and on-page GEO auditing without an enterprise contract.

## Pricing & trial

Product-led with a 7-day / 3-prompt free trial (no card required) converting to self-serve paid tiers from $29/month, with sales-only Enterprise. [Pricing](https://otterly.ai/pricing).
