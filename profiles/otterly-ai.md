## The Solution & Approach

OtterlyAI is a bottom-up, product-led AI-search monitoring platform that positions itself as "Semrush for AI search." It tracks where a brand shows up, what's said about it, and which sites get cited across six AI search engines: ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Google AI Mode, Perplexity, Gemini, and Microsoft Copilot. Its GEO Audit scores 25-plus on-page factors, including content, page speed, structured data, and AI readiness, tying monitoring to on-page optimization.

The angle is distribution and price. Otterly entered the market through an incumbent rather than against it, launching inside the Semrush App Center in January 2025, and it sits as the bootstrapped self-serve floor of the AEO category against venture-funded peers Profound and Peec AI. Growth is PLG: a 7-day, 3-prompt free trial feeds self-serve paid tiers from $29 per month, with sales-only at the Enterprise band.

Otterly pushes a research-led category POV. Its studies found that 95% of AI search citations draw from third-party sources rather than brand-owned content, and that YouTube is the #2 social platform for AI citations behind Reddit. It pairs that intelligence with workflow partnerships, including Noble for media outreach, framing citation data as something to act on rather than just report.

## Best for

SEO and growth teams that want affordable, self-serve AI-search monitoring tied to on-page optimization, without enterprise sales or a VC-priced contract.

## Company, Financials & Funding History

Founded in 2024 in Vienna, Austria by Thomas Peham (CEO, ex-Storyblok VP Marketing and first growth hire at Usersnap), Josef Trauner (ex-Usersnap CEO), and Klaus-M. Schremser (ex-Usersnap head of growth, who runs the GroWTF incubator that backed Otterly). Trauner and Schremser sold Usersnap to saas.group in 2023, and the company has grown from 1,000 users at its December 2024 stealth exit to 30,000-plus by May 2026.
