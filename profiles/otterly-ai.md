## The Solution & Approach

OtterlyAI is a bootstrapped, product-led AI-search monitoring platform that positions itself as "Semrush for AI search." It tracks where a brand appears, what is said about it, and which sites get cited across six AI search engines: ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Google AI Mode, Perplexity, Gemini, and Microsoft Copilot. It sits in the AEO/GEO category as the bootstrapped self-serve floor against venture-funded peers Profound and Peec AI.

The product ties monitoring to action through a GEO Audit that scores 25-plus on-page factors, including content, page speed, structured data, and AI readiness, then generates prioritized fix lists. In 2026 it extended toward a programmatic surface with a Public API, a packaged Claude Skill, and a Marketplace of 101-plus marketing workflows. An MCP server has been announced but had not yet shipped as of June 2026.

Otterly's distribution strategy is to ride incumbents rather than fight them, launching inside the Semrush App Center in January 2025 and partnering with Storyblok and Press Ranger. Its published research pushes a clear POV: 95% of AI search citations draw from third-party sources rather than brand-owned content, which reframes AI visibility as an earned-media and PR problem as much as an on-page one.

## Best for

Self-serve teams, SEO professionals, and agencies that want affordable AI-search visibility monitoring with on-page optimization guidance, without enterprise pricing or a sales process.

## Pricing & trial

Self-serve paid tiers run from $29/month (Lite) to $489/month (Premium), with a sales-only Enterprise band, after a 7-day, 3-prompt free trial that requires no card; there is no free plan. [Pricing](https://otterly.ai/pricing).

## Company, Financials & Funding History

OtterlyAI was founded in 2024 in Vienna, Austria, by Thomas Peham (CEO, formerly Storyblok VP Marketing and the first growth hire at Usersnap), Josef Trauner (ex-Usersnap CEO), and Klaus-M. Schremser (ex-Usersnap head of growth, who runs the GroWTF incubator that backed Otterly). Trauner and Schremser had sold Usersnap to saas.group in 2023, and the fully bootstrapped company grew from roughly 1,000 users at its December 2024 stealth exit to 30,000-plus active users by May 2026.
