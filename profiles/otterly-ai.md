## The Solution & Approach

OtterlyAI is a product-led AI-search monitoring platform that positions itself as 'Semrush for AI search.' It tracks where a brand shows up, what's said about it, and which sites get cited across six AI search engines: ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Google AI Mode, Perplexity, Gemini, and Microsoft Copilot. Its AEO/GEO audit scores 25+ on-page factors (content, page speed, structured data, AI readiness) to tie monitoring to actionable optimization.

The angle is distribution through an incumbent rather than against it. Otterly launched inside the Semrush App Center in January 2025, reaching the SEO incumbent's installed base via single sign-on. It crossed 10,000 users in September 2025 and reported 30,000+ active users by May 2026, all while fully bootstrapped with no external funding sought.

The category POV comes from CEO Thomas Peham: 'When people search with AI, they're not clicking links anymore. They're reading answers.' Within AEO, Otterly plays the bootstrapped self-serve floor against venture-funded peers Profound and Peec AI.

## Best for

SEO and marketing teams that want self-serve AI-search visibility tracking at the low-cost end, especially existing Semrush users.

## Pricing & trial

Self-serve PLG from $29/month (Lite) up to $489/month (Premium), with sales-only Enterprise; no free plan, but a 7-day / 3-prompt free trial with no card required. [Pricing](https://otterly.ai/pricing).
