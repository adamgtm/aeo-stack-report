## The Solution & Approach

Ahrefs is an established SEO platform (Site Explorer, Keywords Explorer, Site Audit, Rank Tracker) that is pushing methodically into the AEO/GEO category from a crawler-infrastructure base. The AEO surface centers on Brand Radar, an AI-visibility index that tracks brand mentions across 6 AI platforms, plus a Custom Prompt Tracking add-on and the free Firehose web-mentions API.

The category POV is stated openly by CMO Tim Soulo: owning the crawler is a structural cost advantage over AI-visibility pure-plays like Profound. His April 2026 long-form piece frames Brand Radar as the smarter choice precisely because the underlying crawl is already paid for, and the free Firehose API leans on the same infrastructure.

The 2026 cadence reads as an incumbent moving outward without abandoning the core. Alongside the AEO additions, Ahrefs shipped a near-daily SEO changelog and a standalone AI marketing agent (Agent A) in May, signaling a bet on agent-driven workflows layered on top of its data.

## Best for

SEO teams that want AI-visibility tracking and agent workflows built on an established crawler and SEO platform rather than an AEO/GEO pure-play.

## Pricing & trial

Tiered SaaS from free Webmaster Tools and a $29 Starter plan up to $1,499/mo Enterprise, with Brand Radar AI Index ($199–$699) and Custom Prompt ($50–$250) add-ons; Agent A is $99/mo with a free one-month trial for existing customers. [Pricing](https://ahrefs.com/pricing).

- **2026-05-28**: Site Explorer's Organic Keywords and Intent charts updated to count every ranking position rather than unique keywords only, improving accuracy for large, multi-position sites. [source](https://ahrefs.com/blog/new-features/)
