## The Solution & Approach

Ahrefs is a bootstrapped, Singapore-headquartered SEO platform built on its own crawler infrastructure, now extending into the AEO/GEO category. The core suite is unchanged: Site Explorer, Keywords Explorer, Site Audit, and Rank Tracker, with a free Webmaster Tools tier and an AI Visibility Checker on top.

The AEO push runs outward from that base. Brand Radar is an AI-visibility index tracking brand presence across 6 AI platforms, sold as a $199 to $699 add-on, with a Custom Prompt Tracking add-on for tracking specific prompts. The free Firehose web-mentions API exposes real-time monitoring on the crawler infrastructure, and an official remote MCP surface went live in April across Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, Claude Code, and Copilot Studio. In May, Ahrefs shipped Agent A, a standalone $99/month AI marketing agent with full access to Ahrefs data.

The category POV comes from CMO Tim Soulo, who frames the bootstrapped model plus owned crawler infrastructure as a structural cost advantage over AI-visibility pure-plays like Profound, arguing Brand Radar is the smarter choice for buyers already inside the Ahrefs data set.

## Best for

SEO teams already on Ahrefs who want AI-visibility tracking layered onto their existing crawler-backed keyword and rank data rather than buying a separate AEO pure-play.

## Pricing & trial

Self-serve tiered subscriptions from a free Webmaster Tools tier through $29 Starter up to $1,499/month Enterprise, with Brand Radar AI-visibility and Custom Prompt add-ons priced separately; annual billing saves up to 17%. [Pricing](https://ahrefs.com/pricing).
