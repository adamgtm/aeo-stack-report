## The Solution & Approach

Ahrefs is a Singapore-headquartered, bootstrapped SEO platform of roughly 196 people, built on the classic Site Explorer, Keywords Explorer, Site Audit, and Rank Tracker surface. Its AEO bet extends outward from that crawler-infrastructure base rather than starting from a clean AI-visibility sheet.

The 2026 cadence pushes into the category from several angles. Brand Radar is an AI-visibility index across 6 AI platforms, paired with Custom Prompt Tracking add-ons (January). The free Firehose web-mentions API followed in March, an official MCP surface across Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, Claude Code, and Copilot Studio in April, and a May run that opened a Rank Tracker public API for Enterprise and added Web Analytics conversion-event tracking.

The category POV is Tim Soulo's: bootstrapped plus owned crawler infrastructure as a structural cost advantage over AI-visibility pure-plays like Profound. That frame, argued directly in his Brand Radar-versus-Profound piece, anchors the whole bet.

## Best for

SEO teams that want AI-visibility tracking layered onto existing crawler and keyword data without paying AEO pure-play prices.

## Pricing & trial

Self-serve, from a $0 free tier (Webmaster Tools) up to $1,499/mo Enterprise, with Brand Radar AI-visibility sold as a $199–$699 add-on and Custom Prompt Tracking at $50–$250; annual billing saves up to 17%. [Pricing](https://ahrefs.com/pricing).
