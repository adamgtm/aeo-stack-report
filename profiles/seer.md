# Seer Interactive

Philadelphia data-driven digital agency founded by Wil Reynolds in 2002, now a 200+ person certified B-Corp serving 130+ enterprise clients at ~92% retention. Seer runs GEO/AEO inside a broader modern-search practice spanning SEO, paid media, analytics, and CRO, backed by a ~15-person AI R&D team building since January 2023.

## The Solution & Approach

Seer treats AI visibility as a measurement problem, not a checklist. A dedicated ~15-person AI team has been building and testing since January 2023, and the firm publishes original research — including a study of brand presence across 231,000+ LLM responses — cited by Forbes, eMarketer, Fast Company, Search Engine Land, and Ahrefs. The work is cross-channel by design: Seer's view is that LLMs weigh your visibility across website, PR, social, reviews, and community, so optimization has to span all of them rather than tune one page. Founder Wil Reynolds, who has spoken at 100+ conferences and is a top-tier voice in the space, frames GEO/AEO/LLMO as the next evolution of the same data-driven search discipline Seer has run for two decades — complementary to SEO, not a clean reset.

## Best for

Enterprise teams that want AI search run as a measured channel alongside SEO, paid, and analytics — buying a long-track-record agency and original research, not a standalone monitoring tool.

## Notable customers

Seer works with emerging-to-Fortune 500 clients across B2B, healthcare, finance, retail, and entertainment; most published case studies are anonymized, so specific logos aren't confirmed here.
