# Limy AI

AI-search measurement and attribution platform that shows how agents discover and recommend a brand, then connects that visibility to traffic, pipeline and revenue.

## The Solution & Approach

Limy combines AI-search analytics, bot-traffic monitoring and attribution. Its platform helps marketing teams understand how their brand appears in AI search and measure what happens after that discovery.

## Best for

Marketing teams that want to connect AI-search visibility and agent traffic to commercial outcomes.
