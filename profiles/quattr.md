## The Solution & Approach

Quattr came up as an SEO platform and added AEO/GEO on top, and that lineage shows. Its pitch is that it is "the only platform that unifies SEO, AEO, and GEO into a single end-to-end workflow" rather than AI tracking bolted onto a rank tracker (vendor claim). The execution angle is the real differentiator: where most AEO tools stop at measurement, Quattr leans on its SEO roots to also restructure pages, auto-link, and ship content, then track citations across Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude, and Bing.

The center of that is GIGA, the company's AI agent. GIGA evaluates content the way AI systems do — scoring structure, authority, internal linking, and discoverability — and surfaces the highest-impact fixes, then drafts and optimizes content against them. In May 2026 Quattr extended GIGA into a Landing Page Generator: feed it a keyword, a reference URL, and product docs, and it returns a deployment-ready page in under fifteen minutes, optimized for both paid-search Quality Score and AI citation visibility. The Autonomous Internal Linking API is the other execution surface — it crawls a site, scores target URLs by business value, and injects links at scale via API, CMS plugins, or edge modules.

So the honest read: Quattr is an execution-led SEO platform that is repositioning hard onto AEO/GEO. That gives it real teeth on the "do the work" side that pure-measurement vendors lack, but on AEO category presence and category-defining POV it is adjacent rather than central.

## Best for

Mid-market and enterprise SEO teams that already think in pages, internal links, and content ops, and want one system that both measures AI visibility and executes the fixes — rather than a measurement-first AEO dashboard.

## Agent Experience

How you build on this platform (or wire it into your own agents):

| Surface | Available | Docs |
|---|---|---|
| API | Yes (deployment / internal-linking; no public dev portal) | — |
| MCP | No | — |
| SDK | Yes (WordPress, HubSpot CMS plugins) | — |
| CLI | No | — |
| llms.txt | No (404) | — |

Quattr's API and SDKs are deployment plumbing — they push optimized internal links and content changes into a CMS at scale, often with a forward-deployed Quattr engineer assisting (per the Kiteworks case study). That is execution-side integration, not a build-on-top platform: there is no public developer documentation portal, no MCP server, no CLI, and `quattr.com/llms.txt` returns 404. Among AEO vendors this is one of the weaker build-on options.
