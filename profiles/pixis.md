# Pixis Visibility

Pixis Visibility is a unified SEO + GEO "operating system" that not only tracks AI-search visibility but executes on it — site audit, content briefs, AI drafting, and one-click WordPress publishing in one loop. It tracks brand citations across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Google AI Overview. It's a newer product line from Pixis, a well-funded AI ad-optimization company (~$209M raised). Self-serve from $99/site/month with a free trial.

## The Solution & Approach

Pixis Visibility's pitch is that most of the category measures and it executes. Where many AEO tools stop at a dashboard — share of voice, prompt-level rankings, sentiment — Pixis carries the loop all the way to a published article: it finds the queries where competitors are cited over you, generates the brief, drafts the content, runs SEO checks, and publishes to your CMS with impact tracking. It runs separate SEO and GEO workflows with independent content calendars that both feed one Execute tab.

The product sits on top of Pixis, a 2018-founded AI marketing-infrastructure company whose core business is codeless ad-campaign optimization. Visibility is a newer line extending that "AI does the work" posture from paid media into organic AI search. The funded parent is the real asset behind it; the AEO product itself is early, and its footprint in our corpus is thin relative to category leaders.

Pixis leans hard on comparison marketing. It publishes head-to-head pages framing itself as the one that "actually executes" against Profound, Semrush, AirOps, Conductor, Evertune, and Bluefish. Treat every one of those as vendor positioning, not neutral fact — the framing is theirs, and the competitors aren't given a say.

## Best for

Teams whose bottleneck is execution, not measurement — small-to-mid marketing orgs on WordPress that want one self-serve tool to find AI-search gaps and ship content against them without stitching together a tracker plus a content stack.

## Pricing & trial

Self-serve from $99/site/month. Engine coverage scales by tier: Starter tracks ChatGPT, Growth adds Gemini, Advanced adds Perplexity, and Enterprise supports custom engines; Google AI Overview monitoring is on every plan. Free trial (no card on Starter and Growth) with 10–15 minute self-serve onboarding. [Pricing](https://pixis.ai/products/pixis-visibility).

## Company, Financials & Funding History

Pixis was founded in late 2018 by Shubham Mishra (Global CEO), Vrushali Prasade (CTO), and Hari Valiyath (CBO), out of the founders' earlier VR work (Tesseract, Norah AI). The core company is a codeless AI infrastructure for ad-campaign optimization and creative generation; Pixis Visibility is a newer AEO/GEO product line on top of it.
