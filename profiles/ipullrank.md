## The Solution & Approach

iPullRank is Mike King's New York technical-SEO agency, repositioned around AI search. Its organizing frame is Relevance Engineering, King's reframing of SEO as an engineering discipline of semantic relevance to retrieval systems rather than keyword mapping. The firm builds the AEO/GEO category through publishing rather than product.

The framework is documented, not just asserted. iPullRank published the AI Search Manual, a 24-chapter public guide it calls the official documentation for Relevance Engineering, open-sourced the Qforia query fan-out tool, and defined a proprietary measurement layer that reframes success from rankings and CTR to citation frequency (Cosine Similarity, Comprehensive Coverage Index, Strategic Entity Richness). The AI Search Strategic Roadmap packages audit findings into a named, year-long service.

The agency runs its own primary research and uses it to hold a technical-authority position in the AEO debate. King's 2024 analysis of the leaked Google Search API documentation, the 'Beyond RAG' argument that every AI search platform is now agentic, and the 'Machine Media: The Death of the Open Web' thesis all push the same view: traditional SEO tooling is obsolete and brands need Relevance Engineering as the operating layer for machine-discoverable content. iPullRank also hosts SEO Week, its annual New York conference.

## Best for

Enterprise and mid-market brands that want a technical-authority partner to run AI-search strategy, audits, and measurement rather than buy a self-serve tool.
