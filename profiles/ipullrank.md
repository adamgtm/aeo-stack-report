# iPullRank

New York enterprise SEO and content-strategy agency founded by Mike King in 2014, now leading the shift to AI search through "relevance engineering." King was named 2025 AI Search Marketer of the Year by Search Engine Land, his second win. Clients include SAP, American Express, Nordstrom, and HSBC.

## The Solution & Approach

Mike King reframes AEO as relevance engineering: the intersection of information retrieval, user experience, AI, content strategy, and digital PR to improve visibility across search and answer engines. iPullRank publishes [The AI Search Manual](https://ipullrank.com/ai-search-manual) and runs SEO Week, and reports more than $4B in documented results for enterprise clients. The bet is depth of technical method over speed.

## Best for

Enterprise and mid-market teams that want a rigorous technical method and a long track record, and are buying senior strategy on top of execution.
