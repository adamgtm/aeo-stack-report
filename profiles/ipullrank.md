## The Solution & Approach

iPullRank is a services firm, not a product. Its organizing frame is Relevance Engineering, Mike King's reframing of SEO as an engineering discipline of semantic relevance to retrieval systems rather than keyword mapping. The framework was introduced publicly at SMX Advanced in June 2025 and formally defined in "An Introduction to the Relevance Engineering Framework" a month earlier.

The agency built its position in AEO through publishing rather than shipping. The AI Search Manual, a 24-chapter public guide billed as "the official documentation for Relevance Engineering in AI Search," came out in August 2025. Qforia, a free Gemini-powered query fan-out simulator, was open-sourced in May 2025. King's May 2024 analysis of the leaked Google Search API documentation, which surfaced siteAuthority and NavBoost, remains the most-cited independent read of that leak. Named services now sit on top of the writing: an AI Search Strategic Roadmap, an AI Search Audit, and a click-to-citation measurement framework built on Cosine Similarity, a Comprehensive Coverage Index, and Strategic Entity Richness.

In July 2026 the frame became the org chart. iPullRank restructured delivery into three client tiers, Emerging, Growth, and Elite, served by Strategic Planning, Content Engineering, Solutions Engineering, Conversation Engineering, Creative, and Measurement Engineering practices. King's POV runs hot on the category: he argues single-shot RAG is obsolete and content must survive five gatekeepers in agentic pipelines, that AI search platforms are branding channels more than performance channels, and that bot traffic will exceed human traffic by 2027. The agency also hosts SEO Week, its annual New York conference, now in its second year.
