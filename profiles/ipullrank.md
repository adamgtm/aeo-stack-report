## The Solution & Approach

iPullRank is a New York technical-SEO agency, now repositioned around AI search. Its organizing frame is Relevance Engineering, Mike King's reframing of SEO as an engineering discipline of semantic relevance to retrieval systems rather than keyword mapping. King introduced the framework at SMX Advanced and the inaugural SEO Week in 2025 and formalized it in a published methodology.

The agency builds the category through publishing rather than software. It maintains the 24-chapter AI Search Manual, positioned as the official documentation for Relevance Engineering, open-sourced the Qforia query fan-out tool, and sells named services including the AI Search Strategic Roadmap and a click-to-citation measurement framework (Cosine Similarity, Comprehensive Coverage Index, Strategic Entity Richness). It also hosts the annual SEO Week conference in New York.

iPullRank runs its own primary research, from the 2024 Google Search API leak analysis to experiments on Gmail as an AI Mode ranking signal, plus provocations like "agentic chunking" and the death-of-the-open-web thesis. That output keeps it positioned as the technical authority on how AI search retrieves content, and places King in the "reset" camp of the AEO debate.
