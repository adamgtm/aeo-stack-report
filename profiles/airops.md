# AirOps

AirOps is an AEO content operations platform for marketing teams, used by Webflow and Chime. It has raised $62.5M and was valued at $225M, with recent launches including AI Ads, AirOps Services, and Quill.

## The Solution & Approach

AirOps combines AI-search measurement with content creation, refresh, research, off-site placement, and campaign execution. Its agents work against a company’s brand guidelines, site structure, performance data, and customer conversations, with human-review checkpoints before publication.

The company has expanded from content workflows into a broader AEO operating layer. Quill converts visibility and citation gaps into campaigns, while Page360 connects AI-search signals with Google Search Console and GA4 data. AirOps Services adds strategists who plan and execute programs across owned content, external placements, AI ads, community, social, and influencers.

Its category position is that visibility scores alone are insufficient. AirOps describes the agent as the brand’s “second customer” and argues that companies must optimize both how agents recommend products and how readily agents can act on them.

## Best for

Marketing teams that want to connect AEO measurement, content operations, off-site visibility, and campaign execution in one platform.

## Company, Financials & Funding History

Founded in San Francisco in 2021 by Alex Halliday, Berna Gonzalez, and Matt Hammel, AirOps began as a data-access product for nontechnical employees. It moved into LLM workflows, narrowed around marketing content, and then expanded into AEO measurement and execution.

| Date | Round | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| 2023-04 | Seed | $7M (Wing Venture Capital), with Founder Collective, XFund, Village Global, Apollo Projects, and Lachy Groom |
| 2024-10 | Series A | $15.5M (Unusual Ventures), $22.5M total raised through Series A |
| 2025-11 | Series B | $40M (Greylock), $62.5M total raised and a $225M post-money valuation |
