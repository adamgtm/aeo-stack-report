## The Solution & Approach

AirOps is the content operations layer inside the AEO/GEO category. Agents generate long-form blog, case study, refresh, and research content against a customer's brand guidelines and site structure, then tie output back to AI search performance. The platform pairs measurement (Page360 unified content performance, prompt tracking across ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews) with placement (AirOps Offsite, which scores publisher influence on AI answers and lands brand mentions on the sites LLMs trust).

The company's POV is that measurement and placement are one company, not two. Its research framing makes the case: AirOps sent 15,000 prompts to ChatGPT, which retrieved 548,534 pages and cited only 15% of them. CEO Alex Halliday positions the May 2026 Quill launch as "your AI agent captain to win AI search" — a buyer-side agent that sees how a brand shows up across AI search, closes gaps, and reports results.

AirOps also pushes a role-naming move: the "Content Engineer," formalized through its AirOps Champions practitioner community. Its argument against fear-driven AEO vendors is that AI search rewards brands with a clear perspective, so taste and judgment are an advantage.

## Best for

Marketing teams that want to operationalize content production and refresh for AI search at scale, from one-person teams up to enterprises with custom agent builds and an embedded content engineer.

## Company, Financials & Funding History

AirOps was founded around early 2022 in San Francisco by Alex Halliday (CEO, ex-Teespring, MasterClass, Bungalow), Berna Gonzalez (CTO, who built the core AI workflow engine), and Matt Hammel (COO, ex-Bungalow). The original product was a no-AI data-access tool that pivoted to LLM workflows after GPT-3/4, then narrowed onto marketing content as marketers became its dominant users.
