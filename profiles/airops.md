## The Solution & Approach

AirOps connects AI-search measurement to content production and distribution. Page360 combines AI citations, Google Search Console, and GA4 data. Quill turns citation issues and sentiment gaps into campaigns, with human review before publication. Brand Kits supply shared context, while CMS integrations connect workflows to publishing.

The scope now extends beyond owned content. Offsite identifies publishers that influence AI answers, AI Ads adds paid placement, and AirOps Services supplies strategy and execution across content, community, social, and influencers. Its stated premise is that the agent is becoming a brand's “second customer.” The resulting offer combines software with the people who operate an AEO program. [Source](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/airopshq_a-new-buyer-journey-requires-a-new-strategy-activity-7503091049435807744-dzbB)

## Best for

Content and growth teams that want to turn AEO findings into repeatable content creation, refresh, and distribution workflows, with optional strategy and execution support.

## Pricing & trial

Solo is free with 100 tracked prompts and 20,000 monthly tasks; Pro is sales-quoted and Enterprise is custom-priced, with no separate free-trial terms stated. [Pricing](https://airops.com/pricing).

## Company, Financials & Funding History

Alex Halliday (CEO), Matt Hammel (COO), and Berna Gonzalez (CTO) founded AirOps in San Francisco, with founding dates reported as 2021 or early 2022. The company began as a data-access tool for non-technical employees, moved into LLM workflows, and concentrated on marketing content as marketers became its dominant users.
