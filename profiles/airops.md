## The Solution & Approach

AirOps is an AI content automation platform for marketing teams, positioned as the content ops layer inside the AEO/GEO category. Agents run long-form blog, case study, refresh, and research workflows against a customer's brand guidelines and site structure, then publish back into the CMS. Native integrations span seven CMS platforms and four project management tools.

The company's thesis is that measurement and placement belong in one product. Its research framing, 15,000 prompts sent to ChatGPT that retrieved 548,534 pages but cited only 15% of them, argues that most content never reaches the answer. The May 2026 Quill launch, billed as an AI agent captain to win AI search, packages prompt mining, measurement, and brand context into one buyer-side agent that finds citation gaps and acts on them.

AirOps also pushes a category POV. It has been building a Content Engineer role and formalized it with the AirOps Champions practitioner community, and it positions AEO against fear-based selling: per CEO Alex Halliday, AI search rewards brands with a clear perspective and something real to say.

## Best for

Marketing teams, including lean or one-person functions, that want to run AI-search content operations at scale against their own brand context and site structure.

## Company, Financials & Funding History

Founded around early 2022 in San Francisco by Alex Halliday (CEO, ex-MasterClass, Bungalow, Teespring), Matt Hammel (COO, ex-Bungalow), and Berna Gonzalez (CTO, who built the core AI workflow engine). It began as a no-AI data-access tool for non-technical employees, pivoted to LLM workflows after GPT-3/4, then narrowed onto marketing content as marketers became its dominant users.
