## The Solution & Approach

AirOps connects AI-search measurement to content production and distribution. Page360 combines AI citations, Google Search Console, and GA4 data. Quill turns sentiment gaps and citation issues into campaigns, with human review before publication. Brand Kits supply shared context, while CMS integrations connect workflows to publishing.

The scope extends beyond owned content. Offsite identifies influential publishers and supports placements, AI Ads adds paid campaigns, and AirOps Services supplies strategy and execution across content, community, social, and influencers. The company now sells both the platform and the work required to operate an AEO program.

AirOps also questions whether visibility alone is sufficient. Its August research found that agents' recommended brands differed from their implementation choices in 232 of 449 matched comparisons. The study measured implementation preference, not completed installations, but establishes the company's stated research direction: what makes products easier for agents to choose and use.

## Best for

Content and growth teams that want to connect AEO measurement with repeatable content creation, refresh, and distribution workflows, with optional strategy and execution support.

## Company, Financials & Funding History

Alex Halliday (CEO), Matt Hammel (COO), and Berna Gonzalez (CTO) founded AirOps in San Francisco, with reported founding dates ranging from 2021 to early 2022. The original product helped non-technical employees access business data, then shifted into LLM workflows and marketing content as marketers became its dominant users.
