Evertune is an enterprise AEO platform connecting AI visibility measurement to affiliate and advertising actions, with customers including Roku and Canada Goose, $19M in disclosed funding, and releases including AI Retargeting, the Visibility Boost ad agent, and Shopping Intelligence.

## The Solution & Approach

Evertune measures brand presence in AI answers and connects visibility gaps to content, affiliate partnerships, and paid media. It repeatedly samples prompts across major AI models, with prompt selection informed by EverPanel, which the company describes as a panel of more than 150 million user prompts.

Its distinguishing angle is media activation. Partner Connect links source analysis to affiliate and publisher campaigns through impact.com and PartnerStack. Visibility Boost targets ChatGPT advertising to conversations where organic recommendations are weak, while AI Retargeting aims to reach category researchers after they leave AI conversations. Both advertising products launched in beta.

Evertune argues that repeated sampling is necessary for reliable AEO decisions. CEO Brian Stempeck has publicly challenged Profound's sampling approach, using polling margins of error to explain Evertune's method. This is the vendor's competitive position, not an independent assessment of either platform.

## Best for

Enterprise brands and agencies that want to connect AI visibility measurement with affiliate partnerships, commerce analysis, and paid media activation.

## Company, Financials & Funding History

Founded in 2024 in New York by Brian Stempeck (CEO), Ed Chater (CPO), and Poul Costinsky (CSO), all former Trade Desk operators. The company expanded from its AI Brand Index measurement product into affiliate activation, shopping intelligence, and advertising.

| Date | Round | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| 2025-12 | Strategic investment | Undisclosed (impact.com), announced alongside the Partner Connect partnership |
| 2025-08 | Series A | $15M (Felicis), bringing disclosed cumulative funding to $19M |
| 2024-10 | Seed | $4M (Eniac Ventures), with NextView Ventures and Roger Ehrenberg participating |
