## The Solution & Approach

Evertune measures how often AI models recommend brands and uses visibility gaps to guide content, partnerships, and advertising. Its measurement approach repeatedly samples each prompt across models, with prompt selection informed by EverPanel, a vendor-described panel of more than 150 million user prompts.

The product connects measurement to media activation. Partner Connect links influential publishers to affiliate, B2B partner, and programmatic campaigns. Visibility Boost targets ChatGPT advertising to conversations where organic recommendations are weak. AI Retargeting, launched in beta in August 2026, extends activation to audiences after they leave AI conversations.

Evertune's category argument centers on sampling rigor. CEO Brian Stempeck argues that repeated sampling produces tighter confidence intervals and more actionable topic-level findings than single prompt runs. His public criticism of Profound is a competitive claim from Evertune, not an independent comparison.

## Best for

Enterprise brand and agency teams that want repeated-sample AI visibility measurement connected to affiliate partnerships, commerce intelligence, and paid media activation.

## Company, Financials & Funding History

Founded in 2024 in New York by Brian Stempeck (CEO), Ed Chater (CPO), and Poul Costinsky (CSO), all former Trade Desk operators. The company expanded from its AI Brand Index measurement product into affiliate partnerships, shopping intelligence, and media activation.

| Date | Round | Amount |
|---|---|---|

| 2025-08 | Series A | $15M (Felicis), bringing disclosed cumulative funding to $19M |
